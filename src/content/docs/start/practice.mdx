From the title screen, choose **Open clinic → Practice charts**. Pick a practice chart. If an exercise is still in progress, choose **Start selected patient** to switch. Read the new intake, then choose **Admit patient** to start the clock. Practice uses its own equipment and supplies. It doesn't affect your campaign, license progress, or shop wallet.

## A short first drill

Use this sequence with a practice chart that describes a minor wound. The example below is a wiki exercise. The body site and supplies depend on the chart you choose.

<Steps>

1. **Find the site.** After admission, use **Body exam** and open **Exam findings**. Match the location in the intake to the finding on the body chart. The patient's right appears on your left.

2. **Finish the dressing.** Select the affected body part and choose **Apply dressing** if the chart offers it. In the window, hold the roll and follow the guide until the progress bar fills. Release the mouse button to fasten it.

3. **Watch, record, and finish.** Look for continued bleeding and check whether the observation progress holds. Choose **Select diagnosis** and use the ending control when it turns green. Review the treatment and outcome, then choose a chart again to repeat or try another one.

</Steps>

<details>
  <summary>The dressing bar is full, but the wound still shows untreated</summary>
  <p>Release the mouse button after completing the wrap. Return to the body chart and read the finding on the same side to confirm the treatment registered.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>The final button is still grey</summary>
  <p>Check for a working diagnosis and completed observation. If the case needs another check, look for the <strong>Me</strong> thought at the lower left and any unavailable action's explanation.</p>
</details>

## Try a second skill

<Aside title="Two more ways to use practice">

**Work a device.** On an equipped chart, check what changes when you connect the ECG, finger probe, or cuff. Start the cuff and look at another body site while it runs.

**Recheck after treatment.** Compare the first exam or device reading with a fresh one, then find both timestamps in **Case review**. Check whether the change lasted before deciding that care is finished.

</Aside>
