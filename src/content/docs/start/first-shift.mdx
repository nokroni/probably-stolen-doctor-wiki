## Open the clinic

<Steps>

1. Start a new game and choose **Doctor**, the green specialization badge with a pale cross. You'll arrive directly at the shop on Day 1. Your starter stock includes the **Doctor license plate** and *How to Be a Doctor*. The handbook fits in the File Box.

2. Find the **BEDSIDE** sign just below the shop’s display window. Inside, **Open equipment rack** lets you see the fitted starter devices. The M2 monitor and portable AED each start with a charged **100-unit Energy Cell**. You can leave the starter setup fitted and return to the counter.

3. Raise the shutter and greet the first visitor. The first appointment is a guided drill with a training manikin. Accept it at the window, then follow the dialogue along the bottom of the bedside screen. A bright border marks the control to use. When the drill ends, return to the counter to finish the conversation.

</Steps>

<Aside title="The license goes in the shop display">

From Day 2, place the **Doctor license plate** directly in the display case to the left of the shutter to receive new patients. The clinic bed and equipment rack can stay in use for anyone you already admitted.

</Aside>

## Receive a patient

Speak to the person at the window. Some customers ask for medicine first. When they mention a symptom, you may be able to ask a follow-up question. If the person who needs care is present and your clinic can help, look for the option to examine or admit them.

Read the intake and the patient's budget before accepting. The bedside timer starts at admission. Start with the immediate problem or use **Body exam** if the patient is stable. After treatment, check how they responded and choose **Select diagnosis** before discharge or handover. Wait for the ending control to turn green.

## Finish the shift

- **Check the receipt.** The amount paid is capped at the budget agreed at the window. The receipt lists the satisfaction-based fee and supplies used from your shop. Shortages and surpluses can change what the next item costs. The manikin drill is free.
- **Hear the reply.** Close the receipt and return to the counter before calling the next visitor. You can open **Case review** to revisit the findings and treatments.
- **Sleep at the end of the day.** Follow the shop's usual overnight flow to save the shift.

### Joining after another specialization

If you already run a shop, look for **Intern Doctor referral** under **Basic → Referrals** in Wild’s Network. Registration costs **100 Favor or Credits**. The follow-up visitor quotes any missing starter equipment, up to **210 Credits**. You can accept or ask them to return in seven in-game days. A guided drill starts your first clinic shift after registration.
