## Open the clinic

<Steps>

1. Start a new game and choose **Doctor**, the green specialization badge with a pale cross. You'll arrive directly at the shop on Day 1. Your starter stock includes the **Doctor license plate** and *How to Be a Doctor*. The handbook fits in the File Box.

2. Find the **BEDSIDE** sign below the shop’s display window. Use **Open equipment rack** to see the fitted starter devices, which are marked **Shabby**. The M2 monitor and portable AED each start with a charged **100-unit Energy Cell**. Select **Return to bedside** to close the rack, then head back to the counter.

3. Raise the shutter and greet the first visitor. The first appointment is a guided drill with a training manikin. Accept it at the window, then follow the dialogue along the bottom of the bedside screen. A bright border marks the control to use. When the drill ends, return to the counter to finish the conversation.

</Steps>

<Aside title="The license goes in the shop display">

From your second clinic shift, place the **Doctor license plate** directly in the display case to the left of the shutter to receive new patients. You can finish caring for anyone already admitted while the plate is out of the display.

</Aside>

## Receive a patient

Speak to the person at the window. Some customers ask for medicine first. When they mention a symptom, you may be able to ask a follow-up question. If the person who needs care is present and your clinic can help, look for the option to examine or admit them.

Read the intake and the patient's budget before accepting. The bedside timer starts at admission. Start with the immediate problem, or use **Body exam** if the patient is stable. After treatment, reassess and select **Select diagnosis** before discharge or handover. If the finish button is dimmed, look beside it to see what still needs doing. Use it once it turns green.

## Finish the shift

- **Check the receipt.** The amount paid is capped at the budget agreed at the window. The receipt lists the service fee and chargeable supplies used from your shop. The fee depends on your license, the type of visit, and satisfaction. Shortages and surpluses can change what the next item costs. The manikin drill is free. [Clinic fees](../../reference/systems/clinic-economy/)
- **Hear the reply.** Close the receipt and return to the counter before calling the next visitor. You can open **Case review** to revisit the findings and treatments.
- **Sleep at the end of the day.** Follow the shop's usual overnight flow to save the shift.

### Joining after another specialization

Call **Joe Wild’s Assistant** on the shop phone and choose **Browse Wild’s Service**. In Wild’s Network, scroll down the **Basic** tab to **Referral** and select **Intern Doctor referral**. Registration has a base price of **100**. Check the displayed quote. Wild’s Favor goes toward the price, and Credits cover the balance.

Open the shop as usual. Joe comes to the window and quotes the tools and supplies you’re missing, up to **210 Credits**. You can accept or ask him to come back in seven in-game days. After registering, put your license plate in the display case and take the manikin drill on your first clinic shift the next day.
