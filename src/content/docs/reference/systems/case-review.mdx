Choose **Case review** after a visit, or open **Recent cases** at the empty bedside and pick a report. The clinic keeps up to 12 completed cases in the recent list. You can review a practice result when the exercise ends.

## Read the timeline

1. Read the outcome at the top of the report or find it in **Disposition**, the final timeline row. Check whether care ended in discharge, handover, crew takeover or another recorded outcome.
2. Select a marker, or move with **Previous event** and **Next event**. **Turning points** is the shorter view. Choose **All events** to include retained body exams, cuff readings and pulse checks.
3. Compare patient findings, treatments and equipment events from the same part of the visit. A bar can show how long CPR, bag-mask support or ventilation lasted.

Nearby entries can share a count marker, such as **×2**. Open it, then choose the record by its time and description.

When you ask a patient to rest, **Treatments** records when you asked. In **All events**, the later **Body exam** has its own timestamp and findings. Select each marker to see when rest was advised and what you found on the recheck.

For transfers with a crew countdown, check **Equipment and crew**. Select **Crew at the door** to see the interval between the call and arrival. If the visit ended while the crew was still on its way, **Crew called** remains the last transport event.

If an action stopped, read the event detail. The journal can distinguish a medicine that was used without effect from a dose that was blocked before use. An event can also show an earlier linked measurement, such as the cuff reading that preceded a suggested pulse recheck. Long visits can omit some earlier details. Older saves may open a summary with fewer records.

## Reopen the evidence

- **Exam findings.** Select the body exam marker, then open **Exam findings** for the saved site-by-site observations. Check its timestamp when comparing it with a later diagnosis or treatment.
- **Rhythm strips.** Use the button when saved ECGs are available. Selecting the linked timeline event first opens the corresponding strip. Compare the displayed timestamps as you move with **Previous strip** and **Next strip**. A referring clinic’s strip can show when you reviewed it and was recorded before arrival.
- **Signed ward update.** After reading the patient's authorized report at the [Night Relief Desk](../referrals/), reopen it above the timeline. Check whether the report describes signed intake or a later update on the patient's condition. The disposition row still shows how your bedside visit ended.
