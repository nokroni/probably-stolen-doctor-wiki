## Grow the clinic crops

Learn both Farmer and Chemist through Wild’s Network to make clinic medicine, or use the corresponding starting specialization for one of those skills. You can buy finished stock while you build the production line.

Add water and power to a native **Hydroponic unit** and plant Pulsevine, Moonmoss, Frostkelp, or Sugarpod. Growth advances overnight. Check the growing plant and the next harvest. Seeds inherit native plant traits, so useful traits and trade-offs persist across generations. Native crop genetics tools can work with these seeds.

## From harvest to medicine

This table describes the game’s fictional ingredients and machine recipes. Use clean cylinders, a blender, and the base game’s chemistry equipment. Open **Medical Supplies (Common or Restricted)** to get one bottle each of **Sterile Clarifier** and **Pulse Stabilizer**. Save a separate pack unopened if you need cartridges.

| Extract for | Main steps in the game |
| --- | --- |
| BroadGuard | Blend Moonmoss. Add acidic solution, then heat. Sterile Clarifier can improve a correctly prepared batch. |
| GlucoRescue | Blend and heat Sugarpod. Filter the resulting debris through clean Kotton Fabric before finishing. Sterile Clarifier is optional. |
| ClearFlow | Blend Frostkelp and Sugarpod together. Prepare a dry residue in the evaporator. The optional Pulse Stabilizer step forms kelp salts before evaporation. |
| PulseLift | Blend Pulsevine, add basic solution, then heat. Pulse Stabilizer can refine a correctly prepared batch. |

Select the matching extract on the **Chemical Finisher**, take out its output, and check the batch. The **Sterile finishing bench** seals an accepted extract into two packs. For a recipe that needs a dry ingredient, check that the vessel has finished losing its residual water. Use the handbook or ingredient tooltip for the game’s quantities and temperature.

Two Frostkelp strips can make two sterile syringes. High-purity PulseLift also makes **PainEase**. At Grade IV it can be combined with a native tilapia skin graft for **Clinic Biogel**, or with ClearFlow for **Heparin**. Read the bench’s purity requirement for each.

## Prepare advanced packs

Each compound uses two matching physical extracts that pass the bench assay, and a full matching bottle. The finished compound takes the lower of the two clinic purities. At Grade IV, one sealed pack of either kind of Medical Supplies makes two **Sterile cartridges**.

| Pack | License | Add to PulseLift extract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressor Bridge | III | ClearFlow extract and a full Sterile Clarifier bottle. |
| Rhythm Buffer | IV | BroadGuard extract, a full Pulse Stabilizer bottle, and one Sterile cartridge. |
| Epinephrine | IV | GlucoRescue extract, a full Sterile Clarifier bottle, and one Sterile cartridge. |

Read the finished pack’s purity and the current response chance at the bedside. If an eligible dose fails, recheck the patient and the updated chance before retrying.

## Power and modules

Use charged base-game **Energy Cells** in powered equipment. Compatible devices can also use a switched-on **Current Converter**. At Grade IV, fit a **Medical power hub** in the rack. Its four slots accept cells, and any one slot can hold a converter. Charged cells split each draw from devices in the same rack. The hub tries the converter if their combined charge is too low. A device uses its own power source if the hub cannot supply the draw. Converters follow the base game's rates, blackouts, and night power rules.

The **M4**, **manual defibrillator**, and **station controller** accept native modules. Check [what each attribute does on your machine](../../items/modules/) before buying one. Before an appointment, check the module bay and the rack’s readiness.
