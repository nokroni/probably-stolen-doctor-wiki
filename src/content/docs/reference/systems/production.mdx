## Grow the clinic crops

Learn both Farmer and Chemist through Wild’s Network to make clinic medicine, or use the corresponding starting specialization for one of those skills. You can buy finished stock while you build the production line.

Add water and power to a native **Hydroponic unit** and plant Pulsevine, Moonmoss, Frostkelp, or Sugarpod. Growth advances overnight. Check the growing plant and the next harvest. Seeds inherit native plant traits, so useful traits and trade-offs persist across generations. Native crop genetics tools can work with these seeds.

## From harvest to medicine

Use clean cylinders, a blender, and [base-game chemistry tools](../../items/chemistry-tools/). Open a Common or Restricted Medical Supplies pack to get both [clinic reagents](../../items/clinic-reagents/), one full bottle of Sterile Clarifier and one of Pulse Stabilizer. When measuring out reagents, set aside full bottles for compounds and keep separate Medical Supplies sealed for cartridges.

| Extract for | Main steps in the game |
| --- | --- |
| [BroadGuard](../../items/broadguard/) | Blend Moonmoss, add acidic solution, and heat. For compounds, add Sterile Clarifier before heating to refine the extract. |
| [GlucoRescue](../../items/glucorescue/) | Blend and heat Sugarpod, then filter out the debris with clean Kotton Fabric. For compounds, add Sterile Clarifier before heating to refine the extract. |
| [ClearFlow](../../items/clearflow/) | Blend Frostkelp and Sugarpod into the same cylinder and dry the mixture in the Evaporator. For compounds, warm it with Pulse Stabilizer to form stabilized kelp salts before evaporation. |
| [PulseLift](../../items/pulselift/) | Blend Pulsevine, add basic solution, and heat. For compounds, add Pulse Stabilizer before heating to refine the extract. |

Open each item page for the amounts, temperatures, and order of steps. Select the matching recipe on the **Chem Finisher**, which uses 2 mL per output. For dry recipes, check that Water has disappeared from the original vessel’s tooltip before finishing. The [Sterile finishing bench](../../items/sterile-bench/) seals an accepted extract into two packs. For compounds, keep the extract from the finisher as an ingredient.

## Prepare advanced packs

The bench requires both Farmer and Chemist and the listed clinic grade. Each craft makes one pack at the lower of the two extracts’ clinic purities. A full carrier bottle must contain 10 mL of the named reagent.

| Pack | License | Add to one refined PulseLift extract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Pressor Bridge](../../items/pressor-bridge/) | III | One ClearFlow extract with stabilized kelp salts and one full Sterile Clarifier bottle. |
| [Rhythm Buffer](../../items/rhythm-buffer/) | IV | One refined BroadGuard extract, one full Pulse Stabilizer bottle, and one Sterile cartridge. |
| [Epinephrine](../../items/epinephrine/) | IV | One refined GlucoRescue extract, one full Sterile Clarifier bottle, and one Sterile cartridge. |

## Power and modules

Use charged base-game **Energy Cells** in powered equipment. Compatible devices can also use a switched-on **Current Converter**. At Grade IV, fit a **Medical power hub** in the rack. Its four slots accept cells, and any one slot can hold a converter. Charged cells split each draw from devices in the same rack. The hub tries the converter if their combined charge is too low. A device uses its own power source if the hub cannot supply the draw. Converters follow the base game's rates, blackouts, and night power rules.

The **M4**, **manual defibrillator**, and **station controller** accept native modules. Check [what each attribute does on your machine](../../items/modules/) before buying one. Before an appointment, check the module bay and the rack’s readiness.
