## Open the clinic

- Choose **Doctor** at the new-game specialization screen to start at the shop with a Grade I plate, handbook, supplies, and fitted starter devices. The first clinic shift begins with a guided manikin drill at the counter.
- If you already run a shop, buy **Intern Doctor referral** in Wild’s Network under **Basic → Referrals**. Registration costs 100 Favor or Credits. The visitor quotes only the missing starter tools and supplies, up to 210 Credits. Accept the quote or ask them to return in seven in-game days.
- After your first clinic shift, put your valid **Doctor license plate** directly in the display case left of the shutter for new clinic appointments. The **BEDSIDE** sign below the window opens the bed. You can finish care for an admitted patient while the plate is out of the display.

## Raise the license grade

The clinic counts settled, paid-care outcomes toward licensing, including eligible local discharges and Medbay handovers. Use the rack’s license information to watch progress. When the licensing officer comes to the window, pay the filing fee or ask them to return in seven days.

| Grade | Completed cases and filing fee | What the grade opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Issued with the specialization or registration | Starter care and diagnosis with the M2, AED, bag and mask, cuff, oximeter, thermometer, and glucose sampler. |
| II | 6 cases · 125 Credits | Rotating equipment supplier, M3, manual defibrillator, 12-lead recorder, and **Complex care referrals**. |
| III | 18 cases · 150 Credits | M4, added diagnostics and support equipment, powered compressions, and additional procedure options. |
| IV | 40 cases · 175 Credits | Station controller, advanced surgery and support, manual dispatch booking, and eligibility for Ward overflow with a paid shop. |

A lost Grade I plate can be replaced through the licensing officer for 20 Credits. At Grades II–IV, check the equipment supplier for a replacement. Put the current plate back in the display after a replacement or promotion.

## Who comes to the window

Clinic visits share the shop’s customer queue. Use the usual bell and read the visitor’s opening request. What the clinic can accept changes with its license, fitted and powered devices, and usable stock. Setting up more equipment and keeping a broader supply of medicines and dressings opens more visit types. Extra referrals use a separate switch and slot.
