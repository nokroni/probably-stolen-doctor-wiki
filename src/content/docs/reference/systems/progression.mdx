## Open the clinic

- Choose **Doctor** at the new-game specialization screen to begin directly at the shop with a Grade I plate, handbook, supplies, and fitted starter devices. The first clinic shift begins with a guided manikin drill at the counter.
- If you already run a shop, scroll to **Referral** on the **Basic** tab of Wild’s Network and choose **Intern Doctor referral**. Registration has a base price of 100. Check the displayed quote. Joe quotes the tools and supplies you’re missing, up to 210 Credits. You can accept or ask him to return in seven in-game days. See [how to register an existing shop](../../../start/first-shift/#joining-after-another-specialization).
- After your first clinic shift, put your valid **Doctor license plate** directly in the display case left of the shutter for new clinic appointments. The **BEDSIDE** sign below the window opens the bed. You can finish care for an admitted patient while the plate is out of the display.

## Raise the license grade

The clinic counts settled, paid-care outcomes toward licensing, including eligible local discharges and Medbay handovers. Use the rack’s license information to watch progress. Reaching a threshold makes the licensing officer eligible to visit from the next day. At the window, pay the filing fee or ask them to return in seven days.

| Grade | Completed cases and filing fee | What the grade opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Issued with the specialization or registration | Starter care with the [M2](../../items/monitors/), [AED](../../items/defibrillators/), bag and mask, cuff, finger probe, thermometer, and glucose sampler. |
| II | 6 cases · 125 Credits | [Medical supplier](../medical-stock/), M3, manual defibrillator, [12-lead recorder](../../items/twelve-lead/), arterial transducer, infusion pump, and **Complex care referrals**. |
| III | 18 cases · 150 Credits | M4, ventilator, blood station, ultrasound, bedside lab, chest compressor, surgery core, and recovery cot. See [diagnostics](../../items/diagnostic-equipment/) and [treatment equipment](../../items/treatment-equipment/). |
| IV | 40 cases · 175 Credits | [Station control module](../../items/station-controller/), shared power hub, microscope cradle, specialist equipment, and [dispatch booking](../referrals/). Ward overflow also requires a paid shop and Complex care referrals. |

A lost Grade I plate can be replaced through the licensing officer for 20 Credits. At Grades II–IV, check the equipment supplier for a replacement. Put the current plate back in the display after a replacement or promotion.

## Who comes to the window

Clinic visits share the shop’s customer queue. Use the usual bell and read the visitor’s opening request. What the clinic can accept changes with its license, fitted and powered devices, and usable stock. Setting up more equipment and keeping a broader supply of medicines and dressings opens more visit types. Extra referrals use a separate switch and slot.
