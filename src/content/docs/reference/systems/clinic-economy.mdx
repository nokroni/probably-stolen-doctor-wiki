## Counter trade

Medicine customers still use the shop’s quote and sale controls. Some patients come to the window on their own and start by asking for medicine. If you can ask about their symptoms, use the reply to decide whether to offer an exam, arrange a private appointment, or continue the sale. A visitor buying for someone at home can take advice or make an eligible purchase. Admission is for a patient who is already there. An ordinary sale uses the shop’s usual stock, payment, and reputation rules.

Some customers comment on the medicine you put on the counter. When choices appear, you can ask a follow-up or choose **Let's talk price on this lot.** Some procurement buyers just ask for a price. During some of these conversations, the open trade panel moves out of the way. It reopens afterward if the same buyer and quote are still active. A panel that started closed stays closed. Check the price before you sell.

After some procurement requests, you can choose **I'll see what I can spare.** or **I can't spare any today.** If the trade panel is open, it moves out of the way while the choices and reply are on screen. When the reply ends, the panel reopens on the original quote if the same buyer and lot are still eligible. To make a sale, confirm it in the shop’s trade panel.

Check the exact item in the quote. For some lots, the buyer may ask for discreet packaging or the doctor may explain the medicine's origin and licensing. These lines can still come up when you switch lots after an earlier quote. If you have no medicine you can sell, you can end the transaction. A buyer can reject the item under their own stock and grade requirements. The medicine’s displayed chemical grade and the percentage used by clinic pack response serve different purposes. A buyer capable of examining a mismatched label may uncover a lower grade through the native trade flow.

## Bills and standing

- Check the payer and spending limit at intake. The bill adds the service fee and eligible clinic stock actually used, up to the agreed budget. A standard paid handover has a base fee of 100, 150, or 200 Credits at satisfaction below 70, from 70 to 89, or from 90. Some charts have their own terms. The manikin drill is free.
- The receipt shows what was used, what was paid, and any uncovered cost. A syringe can be charged alongside its dose. A dose that fails still uses stock. Prepaid supplies brought by a sponsor cost the clinic zero.
- The game values each item when you use it. A shortage or surplus affecting that item can change the charge. A completed paid visit eligible for faction standing normally grants 1, 2, or 3 points at the same satisfaction thresholds. Private visits that suppress standing and unpaid visits grant no clinic standing.
- Read the payer and consent choices before admitting. If the patient can choose between self-pay and a voucher, compare the budgets and read what the voucher copy shares. Where offered, that copy gives the payer the date and charge. Authorization to share clinical details or a later ward update is a separate decision.

## Restock and store supplies

Check [Medical suppliers and crates](../medical-stock/) for equipment offers, pharmacy stock, and ways to get a medical crate.

Usable medicine can be drawn from owned shop storage, including unlocked extensions, the display, and the **Medicine cabinet**. Keep supplies accessible and move finished medicine out of machine outputs before using it at the bed. Check that the chosen lot and its price still match before committing the action.
