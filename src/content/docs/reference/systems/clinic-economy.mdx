## Counter trade

Medicine customers still use the native quote and sale controls. Doctor-specific lines can turn an eligible request into a conversation, an exam, or a private appointment. If the patient has not come in, discuss what the customer can do next or continue the eligible sale. An ordinary sale keeps its own stock, payment, and reputation rules.

Check the exact item in the quote. Some lots need a disclosure about their source or licensing. A buyer can reject the item under their own stock and grade requirements. The medicine’s displayed chemical grade and the percentage used by clinic pack response serve different purposes. A buyer capable of examining a mismatched label may uncover a lower grade through the native trade flow.

## Bills and standing

- The patient or another named payer states the budget at intake. For completed paid care, add the satisfaction-based service fee and eligible consumed supplies, then limit the charge to that budget.
- The receipt shows what was used, what was paid, and any uncovered cost. A syringe can be charged alongside its dose. Stock spent on a failed dose still counts. Sponsored stock supplied by the payer is excluded from the clinic’s consumables charge.
- Prices follow the native item values at use, including relevant shortages or surpluses. A private chart can keep faction standing out of the transaction. Where standing applies, patient satisfaction affects the gain.
- Read the payer and consent choices on the chart. In eligible voucher cases, the billing copy can go to the sponsor with the date and amount. The chart handles medical details and authorization for later updates separately.

## Restock and store supplies

From Grade II, the medical supplier brings a rotating selection appropriate to your license. Medicine dealers and the nighttime shelf also carry qualifying supplies. A **Medical Bay Supply Crate** opened with the matching access card can roll equipment ahead of its normal purchase grade. High-grade equipment is rarer.

Usable medicine can be drawn from owned shop storage, including unlocked extensions, the display, and the **Medicine cabinet**. Keep supplies accessible and move finished medicine out of machine outputs before using it at the bed. Check that the chosen lot and its price still match before committing the action.
