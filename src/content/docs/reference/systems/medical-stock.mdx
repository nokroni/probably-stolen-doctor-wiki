Use the counter for rotating stock, the [Night Relief Desk](../referrals/) for its separate shelf and holds, and a Medical Bay Supply Crate for a chance at equipment ahead of your current purchase grade.

## Buy at the counter

| Source | When to look | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical supplier | From [Grade II](../progression/). Leave space on the counter and call the visitor when they join the shop queue. | Two rotating equipment offers within your grade, plus one or two supplies. On the first cart after promotion, the featured device is the [M3](../../items/monitors/), [M4](../../items/monitors/) or [station control module](../../items/station-controller/) for your new grade. |
| Pharmacy and other eligible medicine sellers | From Grade I. Finish the seller’s opening dialogue and inspect the items on the counter. | [Sealed clinic packs](../../items/clinic-medicine/) and sterile syringes. Grade III opens [Blood Bags](../../items/blood-station/), Heparin and Pressor Bridge. Grade IV adds Rhythm Buffer and Epinephrine to the rotation. |
| Retired farmer and retired chemist | After unlocking the relevant [production skills](../production/). | The farmer offers [clinic seeds and spores](../../items/clinic-crops/). The chemist can offer the [Sterile finishing bench](../../items/sterile-bench/) when both Farmer and Chemist are unlocked. |

Browse what the equipment supplier brought that visit. Buying sealed medicine requires no Farmer or Chemist unlock. If you’re buying for a case, check the required item and whether you still have one in stock.

## Open a Medical Bay Supply Crate

1. Look for the sealed **Medical Bay Supply Crate** from a Thief or check the **Underground Exchange**. When the medical crate offer is available, the exchange takes four **Disposable Keycards (Medical)**. Set aside an opening keycard before trading.
2. Use a **Disposable Keycard (Medical)** on the sealed crate. Check its actual contents. Any specialization can receive the clinic loot.
3. Move any recovered device to the [bedside equipment rack](../../items/equipment-rack/) and power it if required. An electronic device in a crate has no battery fitted. You can use recovered equipment ahead of its usual purchase grade when the setup supports it. The license still determines which appointments and purchases are unlocked.

At ordinary luck, about one in three crates attempts to add equipment. If it does, the grade is selected using the table below. A separate one-in-four roll can add a standard clinic medicine pack, sterile syringe or spare bedside tool. The item needs room in the crate.

| Equipment grade | Share of equipment rolls | Possible clinic equipment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 70% | M2 with pressure cuff, or portable AED |
| II | 23% | M3, manual defibrillator, 12-lead recorder, arterial transducer or infusion pump |
| III | 6% | M4, transport ventilator, blood station, portable ultrasound, surgery core, bedside lab or recovery cot |
| IV | 1% | Station control module, Immune Regulation Module or microscope cradle |
