## Daytime referrals

- **Complex care referrals** costs 350 in Wild’s Network and requires Grade II. Buying it turns on the **Extra patient** switch at the empty bedside. When the switch is on, an eligible day can get one extra appointment after the ordinary clinic visit. The patient must still match your equipment and supplies.
- **Ward overflow referrals** costs 2,000 in the advanced section. It requires Grade IV, a fully paid shop, and **Complex care referrals**. Once unlocked, cycle the dispatch titles at the empty bed to **Ward overflow**, then choose **Book dispatch**. Dispatch selects a case your fitted equipment and available stock can support. If the button shows **No matching call**, your setup currently matches none of the available cases. For other dispatches that show **Practice call**, complete the rehearsal first. A booked visit can arrive from the next day with the plate on display and the required setup ready. Use **Cancel booking** or defer at the window to change the arrangement.
- The overflow fund adds 150 Credits to the fee for completed care and caps the payment, supplies included, at 550 Credits. Read the chart’s transfer choices before committing to a demanding visit.

## Nighttime services

After buying the introduction, choose the **Night Relief Desk** on the after-hours transit map. Finish active care before traveling. If the station is locked down or the lift is under maintenance, check the notice and return on an open evening.

| Wild’s Network card | Cost and requirement | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night Desk introduction | 180 · Doctor license | Visit the desk and shop its small rotating shelf. |
| Dispensary reserve | 280 · introduction | Request one supply family, then pay on pickup. A supply shortage can delay the hold. The slip shows the remaining pickup window. |
| Community clinic contacts | 425 · introduction and Grade II | Confirm an eligible community referral through the desk. The request can wait until your clinic is ready. |
| Ward correspondence | 550 · introduction and Grade III | Request and read a signed update when the patient authorized it and the ward has replied. |
| Mutual-aid supply agreement | 900 · introduction, Grade IV, paid shop, and Ward overflow | Accept a sponsored referral with one prepaid supply. The clinic only bills its own consumed stock. An unused sponsored item returns to the sponsor. |

## At the desk

The window stays open throughout your visit. **Return to shop** is the only exit. If you leave items on the ground, the game warns you before departure. Cancel the warning to stay and pick them up.

- **Shelf.** Check the supply and current quote, then choose **Pick up supplies**. It goes into a free slot in your worn bag or a pocket. A medical shortage can leave the shelf empty.
- **Reserve.** You can have one hold at a time. Request dressings or sealed antibiotics. Grade IV also allows a request for restricted medical supplies. Pay on pickup. Once the delivery is ready, you get two open pickup nights. A delay or closure can hold off the start of that window. You can cancel an outstanding order. After collecting one, open the shop three more times before requesting the next.
- **Referrals.** Confirm an available appointment or an offered follow-up slip. The visit can take place from the next day. If your clinic is missing required stock or equipment, the desk holds the booking until you can take it.
- **Ward.** Pick the relevant slip and read the transfer reply. With **Ward correspondence**, use **Request a signed ward update** when it is available. The patient must have authorized the update and the ward must have signed it. A signed intake confirms what the ward received. Check a later signed entry for news of the patient's condition. After reading the update at the desk, you can reopen it in [Case Review](../case-review/). If the update includes a check-in request from the patient, choose **Book the requested check-in** to arrange a free chat at the counter from the next shop opening.
- **Mutual aid.** Read which supply is included before booking. The clinic still needs the rest of the listed equipment and stock. The sponsor’s item is for this visit and does not go into your sellable stock. It goes back unused after care. Completed paid care gets the same 150 Credit supplement and 550 Credit budget as Ward overflow.
