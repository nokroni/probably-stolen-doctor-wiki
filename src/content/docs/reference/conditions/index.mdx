---
title: Conditions and symptoms
description: Look up findings, playable conditions, and treatment paths in the Doctor mod.
---

Use [Symptoms and first checks](./symptoms/) when you have a complaint or a monitor finding. Open a topic below when you want to compare diagnoses or understand why an intervention is available.

These conditions and actions are part of the Doctor mod. Use the bedside chart to see when the game needs a repeat finding, observation, local discharge, or handover.

| Area | What to look up |
| --- | --- |
| [Chest pain](./chest-pain/) | Reflux, chest wall pain, acute coronary syndrome, heart attack, and suspected lung clot |
| [Heart rhythms and arrest](./rhythms/) | Fast or slow rhythms, VF, VT, SVT, AF, asystole, pulseless electrical activity, and recurrent collapse |
| [Breathing problems](./breathing/) | Asthma, allergies, inhalation injury, assisted breathing, and equipment faults |
| [Fever and infection](./infection/) | Wound and implant infection, pneumonia, kidney infection, and circulation in systemic infection |
| [Neurologic symptoms](./neurologic/) | Headache, brief fainting, abnormal speech or movement, low glucose, and stroke |
| [Abdominal pain](./abdominal/) | Stomach upset, gastroenteritis, dehydration, and suspected appendicitis |
| [Metabolism and exposure](./metabolic-exposure/) | Temperature, electrolytes, radiation, power hazards, and reactions to substances |
| [Wounds and limb injuries](./wounds/) | Cuts, burns, debris, sprains, dislocation, and fractures |
| [Major trauma](./major-trauma/) | Significant blood loss, penetrating injury, chest air or blood, and severed digits or limbs |
| [Recovery and specialist care](./recovery-specialist/) | Repeat checks after moving a patient, healed scar review, specialist circuit care, and follow-ups |

Ask about onset or check the body site before committing to a working diagnosis. Low pressure, a quiet alarm, and an organized ECG can each coexist with ongoing problems. For the controls and required stock, see [Procedures](../procedures/) and [Items and equipment](../items/).
