---
title: Neurologic symptoms
description: Use history, speech, limb findings and glucose to assess headache, fainting and focal deficits.
---

Ask when the patient was last acting normally. Check speech, smile, arm hold and grip. The **Body exam** records findings at its actual time. Use a fingertip glucose check on a usable hand when indicated. Repeat both the reading and the movement check after correction.

| Condition | Findings and next steps in the game |
| --- | --- |
| Recurrent headache | A gradually developing, familiar headache with stable speech and movement can be monitored. Use **Headache under assessment** while gathering findings. Offer **Quiet rest** when eligible, then recheck the headache, speech and arms before discharge. |
| Sudden severe headache or suspected intracranial bleeding | Record a headache that peaks almost immediately, even if speech and arm hold look normal at first. A newly drifting arm, uneven smile or increasing drowsiness adds concern. Use the offered support and **Call Medbay**. |
| Low blood sugar | Sweating, pallor, shaky hands, weakness, confusion or slurred speech. Check glucose, confirm **GlucoRescue** when offered, then let the reading change and recheck the original deficit. |
| Suspected stroke | Compare both sides. Weakness or speech changes may persist after the repeat glucose is back in range. Include the time last acting normally and the repeat findings in the handover. If a glucose check is unavailable, finish **Body exam** and select a hand. Use **Refer without glucose test** when it appears and Medbay can finish the check. |
| Brief fainting | Ask what happened before the patient went down. Use **First aid?** if offered. Examine for fall injuries, check fingerstick glucose and take a fresh cuff pressure. Let the patient sit a moment, then use **Check for dizziness**. |
| Drug reaction | Shallow breathing or slowed responses can look like a neurologic deficit. Examine, review available exposure evidence and see [Metabolism and exposure](../metabolic-exposure/). |

## Blue Blood and scan review

Check speech and movement and use a current glucose reading to rule out low blood sugar. If the head menu offers **Medbay scan review**, request it for more information. From Grade III, some in-game ischemic stroke charts offer **Blue Blood** after the bedside checks, even while scan results are pending. Power the fitted Blood station, select a usable forearm, and confirm an owned Blue Blood bag. Repeat speech and arm checks and report any remaining deficit.
