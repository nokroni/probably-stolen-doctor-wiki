An organized waveform can coexist with no felt pulse. Compare the wrist, readable ECG, and a current pressure when the game can measure one. A lost lead or probe needs its own check.

In the game, the wide beats of VT can have a broad upright peak, a deep downstroke, or a double peak with a notch. Another pattern rises and falls continuously, with rounded troughs and slightly sharper peaks, like a repeating V–U–V.

| Finding | In-game response |
| --- | --- |
| **Slow arrhythmia** with dizziness or low pressure | Check the ECG and repeat a usable pressure. On eligible charts, **PulseLift** becomes available after the exam and low-pressure evidence. Continue looking for a coronary, cold or metabolic cause. |
| **Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT)** or **atrial fibrillation (AF)** with a pulse | A regular rapid run with narrow complexes suggests SVT. An irregular narrow trace and a peripheral pulse slower than the ECG suggest AF. Check the wrist and pressure. **SYNC** depends on the indication and a readable ECG. A stable AF visit can call for monitoring and handover. |
| Ventricular tachycardia (VT) or ventricular fibrillation (VF) | VT appears as wide complexes in regular runs or with changing shapes. VF appears disorganized. Check the wrist, attach pads at the chest and follow the AED advice or manual prompt. In the game, the manual shock mode for VT and VF is unsynchronized. |
| Asystole or organized electrical activity with no pulse | Use eligible airway support and CPR. Each Space tap counts for manual compressions. A slow escape rhythm can appear during recovery. Check the cause and confirm a return of pulse before switching to observation. |
| Weak or absent circulation after a shock | Recheck the wrist when free, then measure pressure if a pulse returns. An organized ECG can appear before the pulse or stable pressure returns. Look for blood loss or another cause and keep the indicated support running. Eligible Grade IV charts can enable **Epinephrine** with current circulation evidence. |
| Repeated VT or VF | Record new rhythm or pressure findings. Check what changed since the last recovery, including coronary care. **Rhythm Buffer** can delay recurrence on eligible charts with a pulse, readable ECG, recent pressure and recorded recurrence. |

## Recheck before completing care

Use an updated diagnosis and repeat readings when requested. A new symptom or deterioration ends the current observation window. Treat the change and allow the restarted observation to finish before local discharge or handover.
