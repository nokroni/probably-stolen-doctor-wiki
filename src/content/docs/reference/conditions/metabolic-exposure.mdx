---
title: Metabolism and exposure
description: Look up electrolyte, temperature, drug, electrical and radiation findings.
---

Read the patient or witness account and inspect any available object involved. When the display shows an active hazard and offers an isolation control, use it before other procedures. A chart may ask you to record an uncertain source.

| Finding or condition | In-game check and response |
| --- | --- |
| Heat illness or cold exposure | Check temperature. Hot skin and confusion point toward heat. Cold wet clothing, shivering or slowed responses point toward cold. Use the offered **Cool patient** or **Warm patient**, then remeasure after it has had time to work. |
| Electrolyte imbalance or missed filtration | With cramps, skipped beats or collapse, read the history and use **Check chemistry**. If the chart shows a filtration card, read it at the indicated site. Some charts allow chemistry and **ClearFlow · Electrolytes** during CPR. Recheck the rhythm and pulse after treatment. |
| Elevated kidney or liver markers | The bedside lab can report elevated organ markers with electrolytes in range. Read both parts of the result. Compare the affected region, symptoms and current circulation before choosing care. Normal electrolytes alone do not clear an injury. |
| Electrical injury | If the warning says the damaged cable is still live, select **Disconnect power cable** in the lower-left bed controls. Then check chest rise, wrist pulse, ECG and pressure as available. Use the game’s offered controls if rhythm or circulation is impaired. |
| Radiation exposure | Read the badge and witness report, match relevant property, and use the offered isolate/seal action. Look for concurrent mechanical or internal injury. Give the crew the findings when the chart asks for transfer. |
| Exposure to a native medicine or substance | Read the wrapper, vial, food container, or witness line. Compare the history with breathing, pulse and glucose. Arrange supported transfer if breathing stays poor or the chart requests it. |
| Substance dependence | During a monitored consultation, discuss a recovery plan after history and exam. Use **Synaprest** if the patient agrees and the chart offers it. Reassess and follow the displayed disposition. |

## Read the label with the exam

- **Oxymore and mixed evidence.** If two containers are present, ask what was actually used and compare the property record. Give **Naloxgone** only after the chart confirms qualifying exposure. Recheck breathing after the dose.
- **Dream Dust, Phanta, Nightmare Dust, Nightmare Cap and Zyanide.** A torn wrapper, drowsiness, strange waking movements or a reported food exposure can guide the history. Keep breathing support and send the matching item or record when the chart requests it. The Oxymore route is only enabled for verified qualifying evidence.
- **Caffeine products or ZeroChew.** Ask about intake and the last meal, then check glucose. Correct a confirmed low reading and recheck the presenting symptom.
