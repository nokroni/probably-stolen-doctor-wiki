---
title: Abdominal pain
description: Compare stomach upset, dehydration, suspected appendicitis and deeper causes.
---

Ask where the pain started, whether it moved, what makes it worse, and whether there is vomiting, diarrhea or trouble drinking. Examine the relevant region. The game can offer a provisional **Abdominal pain, cause unclear** diagnosis while you work.

| Presentation | What separates it | In-game care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stomach upset or gastroenteritis | Intermittent cramping, vomiting or loose stools, diffuse soreness without one persistent tender spot. | Offer water and rest when enabled. After observation, recheck the abdomen and whether the patient can keep water down, then discharge if ready. |
| Dehydration | Dry mouth, dizziness, weak pulse or inability to keep a drink down. | Compare pressure, pulse and oral tolerance. Rest and water suit the simple complaint. A chart with persistently low pressure can require monitored fluid support. |
| Suspected appendicitis | Pain moves from around the belly button to the lower right, worsens with motion, and remains focal on exam. | Report the focal pain and call Medbay. A limited ultrasound result does not clear that finding. |
| Suspected kidney infection | Pain at the flank or below the ribs, painful urination, shaking, vomiting. | See [Fever and infection](../infection/) for the treatment check and transfer route. |
| Internal or penetrating abdominal injury | Impact or embedded object with increasing tenderness, distention, weak pulse or falling pressure. | Scan the affected region as offered, support circulation, and address the source or arrange handover. See [Major trauma](../major-trauma/). |
