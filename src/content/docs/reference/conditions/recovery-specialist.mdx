A chart can show new findings after moving a patient or changing support. Read the last recorded time. Measure the patient now and use the current result for signing or disposition.

| Situation | What to do in the game |
| --- | --- |
| Sensor fault after moving to a cot | Check wrist and chest movement first. Refit the indicated sensor. Repeat the cuff if the previous reading predates the move or has expired. |
| Worsening breathing after moving or reconnecting | Check actual chest rise, reconnect or refit the circuit when needed, and use the chart's breathing support. Continue watching the fresh pressure and reading. |
| Healed scar consultation | Examine the site and ask consent. Use the **Mark scar outline** window only when offered. Confirm the accepted mark, defer, or use the licensed surface-revision path. Finish the requested recovery check. |
| Follow-up after local treatment | Read the past finding, ask whether symptoms changed, repeat the requested examination and record new findings against the earlier result. |
| Review after disposition | **Case review** shows the timeline. Use it to find deterioration, treatments, reassessments and interruptions to observation. Use **Recent cases** to reopen a filed chart. |

## Implant findings

Examine the affected area. Inspect the implant service port if the chart offers that action. Use what you find to decide which other checks or support the patient needs.

| Finding | In-game checks and care |
| --- | --- |
| Hot implant rim, foul dressing or drainage | Check the site and temperature. Use the offered antimicrobial treatment and reassess breathing and pressure if the patient worsens. See [Fever and infection](../infection/). |
| Dry graft seam with swelling or deep tenderness | Scan the affected region when offered and compare with the pulse and pressure. The chart may call for blood support, repair or monitored transfer. See [Major trauma](../major-trauma/). |
| Implant port complaint with rapid beats | Check ECG, wrist pulse and current pressure. Use the rhythm controls the device enables for the observed rhythm. See [Heart rhythms and cardiac arrest](../rhythms/). |
| Whistling airway insert or fading chest rise | Check the chest and fitted breathing circuit. Reseat the connection or provide breathing support when offered, then recheck chest rise and a current reading. See [Breathing problems](../breathing/). |

## Specialist treatment

When a condition requires specialist treatment, read the bedside chart to check the maintenance medicine, equipment, and supplies. If the chart offers an implant procedure, follow its requirements and repeat the requested checks after the procedure. Read the latest monitor and observation results before signing. See the [Immune Regulation Module](../../items/immune-regulator/) for equipment.
