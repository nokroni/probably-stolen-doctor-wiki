A chart can show new findings after moving a patient or changing support. Read the last recorded time. Measure the patient now and use the current result for signing or disposition.

| Situation | What to do in the game |
| --- | --- |
| Sensor fault after moving to a cot | Check wrist and chest movement first. Refit the indicated sensor. Repeat the cuff if the previous reading predates the move or has expired. |
| Worsening breathing after moving or reconnecting | Check actual chest rise, reconnect or refit the circuit when needed, and use the chart's breathing support. Continue watching the fresh pressure and reading. |
| Healed scar consultation | Examine the site and ask consent. Use the **Mark scar outline** window only when offered. Confirm the accepted mark, defer, or use the licensed surface-revision path. Finish the requested recovery check. |
| Advanced implant or systemic care | Review the affected connection, supported clinical findings and the specialist bed equipment. Some sequences have distinct treatment, transfer and signed observation phases. Check the device, patient and fresh readings at each phase. |
| Follow-up after local treatment | Read the past finding, ask whether symptoms changed, repeat the requested examination and record new findings against the earlier result. |
| Review after disposition | **Case review** shows the timeline. Use it to find adverse changes, treatments, reassessments, and interrupts to observation. Use **Recent cases** to reopen a filed chart. |
