Take a temperature and locate the source. A hot wound, coarse breathing on one side and flank tenderness lead to different checks. Reassess the patient’s current breathing and circulation after treating the source.

| Playable pattern | Findings and checks | Care and disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Localized infection** at a wound | Examine the site for redness, heat, swelling, drainage or tenderness. Check heat exposure if it started as a burn. | **Dress infected wound** or **Cover burn** as offered. Use **BroadGuard** or eligible **Phagimycin** where the chart supports it. Recheck the site after local care. |
| Deep infected swelling | A puncture may look closed with a tender swelling underneath. | Scan the indicated region if the chart needs imaging, and report the deeper finding. Use the allowed medication and refer if source care remains. |
| Systemic infection or septic shock | Chills, marked weakness, weak pulse, falling blood pressure, or increasing respiratory support. | Treat the source and use indicated antimicrobial and pressure support. **PulseLift** can be a bridge after a recent low reading. A powered infusion can be needed where the chart supports it. |
| Pneumonia | Wet cough, fever, being winded at rest, or coarse sounds more on one side. | Record cough history, chest findings and vitals. Use breathing support and indicated medicine on equipped visits. Arrange Medbay care when the chart calls for it. |
| Suspected kidney infection | Flank pain below one side of the ribs, shaking, painful urination or vomiting. An ultrasound can show no obvious obstruction while infection remains possible. | Ask about urinary symptoms and examine the abdomen. BroadGuard can be offered, and a low pressure reading can make ClearFlow available. Send the flank and urinary findings to Medbay. |
| **Implant complication** | Heat, foul dressing or fluid around a port. Airway or rhythm changes can accompany an implant complaint. | Examine the affected area and inspect the port. Treat infection or stabilize the other failing system as indicated by the chart. |

A falling pressure or worsening breathing resets the watch. If pressure improves during an infusion but oxygen falls, recheck chest rise and SpO₂ and use the offered breathing support. A new irregular rhythm can accompany severe chest infection. Keep treating the infection and supporting breathing, then compare a fresh tracing and pulse.
