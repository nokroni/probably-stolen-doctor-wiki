---
title: Breathing problems
description: Use chest movement, lung findings and connected signals to distinguish breathing problems.
---

At the chest, read the left and right findings. Check chest movement, ask about cough or exposure, and inspect the mask or line if fitted. A SpO₂ number only helps when the clip signal is usable. With an M4 and a connected CO₂ line, compare the capnography shape and chest motion over several breaths.

| Condition or fault | What to look for | In-game actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Respiratory illness | Cough with easier breathing and an overall steadier exam. | Recheck after rest when offered. Compare temperature, lung findings and breathlessness to decide whether the chart is ready to discharge. |
| Asthma exacerbation | Wheeze on both sides, difficulty speaking in full sentences, sometimes a fin-shaped CO₂ trace. | Ask about exposure and support breathing. Some charts enable the game's **PulseLift** pack at a forearm after the exam. Recheck whether breathing stays improved. |
| Allergic reaction | Rash or welts with hoarseness, wheeze or a weakening pulse. | Examine, support breathing and check pressure. Use the game's **PulseLift** option when offered. If the chart calls for transfer, keep support in place until crew takeover. |
| Inhalation injury or breathing failure | Smoke or irritant history, shallow breathing, poor chest rise, or trouble remaining alert. | Fit the bag and mask or powered ventilation when available. Repeat checks after a change, including CO₂ where fitted. |
| Pinched or detached circuit | Poor chest rise or a collapsed bag after a clear change in the gear. | Open **Reseat circuit** or **Refit mask** when offered. Confirm renewed chest rise before trusting a new number. |
| Probe fault | A missing or implausible reading with a felt pulse and continuing movement. | Read the actual patient, refit the finger clip and take another pressure when needed. |
| Cough with fever and focal coarse breath sounds | Compare with [pneumonia](../infection/). A traumatic cause with uneven rise is covered under [Major trauma](../major-trauma/). | Document both sides. Use the indicated support and disposition after reassessment. |
