Start with **Body exam**. Click the injured site, then read the exact location and whether anything is still covered. The patient's right appears on your left. A treatment applies to the selected body part only.

| Finding | In-game action and recheck |
| --- | --- |
| **External wound** | A cut, scrape, or accessible puncture can bleed at the surface. Use **Hold pressure** when shown. Choose **Apply dressing**, follow the circular guide and release when prompted. Recheck the same site. |
| Loose grit or shards | Use **Clear loose debris** if offered and move the pictured pieces to the tray. Then recheck and dress the injury. |
| Hot contact injury or burn | Use the indicated **Cover burn** action and an eligible dressing or Fish skin. If the area is hot, swollen or draining days later, compare [wound infection](../infection/). |
| Wrist sprain, shoulder strain or chest wall strain | Ask how it happened. Examine for deformity and check the indicated movement or distal circulation. Rest and recheck when the local chart permits it. |
| Fracture | Open **Apply splint**, place both illustrated bone segments and the plate as the panel shows, and repeat the distal circulation check if requested. Treat each site separately. |
| Dislocated joint | Open **Reduce joint**, move the joint illustration to its guide, and release on the marked position. Recheck distal findings before disposition. |
| **Wound aftercare** | Read the earlier findings, ask about changes, and re-examine the same location before closing the follow-up. |

Use the chart’s local discharge once care, diagnosis and any observation are complete. New swelling, faint pulse or a deteriorating pressure may need more than local dressing. See [major trauma](../major-trauma/) and [wound care controls](../../procedures/wound-care/).
