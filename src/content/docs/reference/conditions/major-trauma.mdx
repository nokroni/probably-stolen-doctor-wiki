---
title: Major trauma
description: Compare external blood loss, deep injuries, chest trauma and traumatic limb loss.
---

Use **Body exam**, then select each affected site. If clothing conceals a wound, use the offered action to open it. A dry dressing or adequate finger-probe reading can coexist with poor circulation. Compare the ECG with a felt pulse and a current pressure after a direct blow to the chest.

| Condition | In-game check and care |
| --- | --- |
| Major external bleeding | Expose the site, hold pressure where offered, and dress it separately from other injuries. Recheck the wrist or appropriate limb and repeat pressure. Start the indicated blood support if the chart and fitted station allow it. |
| Blunt internal injury or penetrating abdominal injury | Examine and scan the source. Protect an embedded object with the offered brace action. Use eligible blood support. If a powered surgery core and stock make local repair available, capture and seal in separate steps. Recheck pressure and any remaining bleeding or transfer requirement. |
| **Chest wall injury** | After a blow to the chest, compare local tenderness with chest rise on both sides. If the scan shows air or blood in the chest, update the working diagnosis to match. |
| Pneumothorax | After chest injury, one side may rise less. The scan may describe absent lung sliding and no visible fluid on that side. From Grade III, **Drain chest** becomes available on supported charts with current pressure and a sterile kit. Follow the game's guide, then recheck chest rise and pressure. |
| Hemothorax | The scan can show fluid above the diaphragm. Drainage can improve breathing while blood loss remains. Follow the applicable bleeding, transfusion or surgical-control prompts. Repeat the pressure afterward. |
| Mixed chest injury and limb bleeding | Scan the affected chest and treat the visible wound as a separate source. Use the provisional diagnosis if offered while checking. When you have a result, match the combined diagnosis to the injured sites and what the scan showed. |
| Traumatic limb loss | The body chart shows the missing part. Dress the stump, manage other sites, and use a viable side for the cuff, pulse and vascular access. Confirm tissue custody if the game requests it. |
| Severed finger | Match the finger and side on the hand card. Check the tissue cassette and consent. Use the enabled replantation procedure if the chart and equipment support it. Use **Check flow at finger** afterward, or dress and transfer with the recorded cassette if needed. |

When a transfusion is indicated, check the selected product in the tray before confirming. **Blood Bag** and **Blue Blood** have distinct uses. The tray shows the current supply quote for the selected bag. If the reserved bag was removed, choose an available replacement. Then recheck circulation after the treatment.
