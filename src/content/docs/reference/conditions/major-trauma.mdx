---
title: Major trauma
description: Compare external blood loss, deep injuries, chest trauma and traumatic limb loss.
---

Check each injured region. A dry dressing or adequate finger-probe reading can coexist with poor circulation. After a direct blow to the chest, also compare the ECG with a felt pulse and current pressure. The heart can pump weakly even after the tracing organizes.

| Condition | In-game check and care |
| --- | --- |
| Major external bleeding, including **Left thigh hemorrhage** | Expose the site, hold pressure where offered, and dress it separately from other injuries. Recheck the wrist or appropriate limb and repeat pressure. Start the indicated blood support if the chart and fitted station allow it. |
| Blunt internal injury or penetrating abdominal injury | Examine and scan the source. Protect an embedded object with the offered brace action. Use eligible blood support. If a powered surgery core and stock make local repair available, capture and seal in separate steps. Recheck pressure and any remaining bleeding or transfer requirement. |
| **Chest wall injury** | After a blow to the chest, compare local tenderness with chest rise on both sides. If the scan shows air or blood in the chest, update the working diagnosis to match. |
| Pneumothorax | After chest injury, one side may rise less. Use a chest scan to distinguish pleural air from blood. From Grade III, **Drain chest** becomes available on supported charts with current pressure and a sterile kit. Follow the illustrated guide, then recheck movement and pressure. |
| Hemothorax | Imaging shows blood in the chest. Drainage can improve breathing while blood loss remains. Follow the separate bleeding, transfusion or surgical control and verify pressure again. |
| Mixed chest injury and limb bleeding | Scan the affected chest and treat the visible wound as a separate source. If offered, record **Chest and thigh trauma, source under review** while checking. Match a combined diagnosis to the scan and wound, such as **Right chest air and left thigh bleeding** or **Right chest blood and left thigh bleeding**. |
| Traumatic limb loss | The body chart shows the missing part. Dress the stump, manage other sites, and use a viable side for the cuff, pulse and vascular access. Confirm tissue custody if the game requests it. |
| Severed finger, shown as **Left index finger severance** on the working diagnosis list | Check the game’s tissue cassette and consent. Use the enabled replantation procedure only if the chart and equipment support it. Use **Check flow at finger** afterward, or dress and transfer with the recorded cassette if needed. |
