---
title: Injury, exposure, and recovery symptoms
description: Wounds, deep injuries, medication exposure, implant problems, and changes after a move.
---

Find the affected site, source, or connection before choosing an action.

<div class="symptom-list">

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Drowsiness after substance use, or a power or radiation warning</summary>
  <p>Follow any on-screen isolation prompt. If there is a wrapper, badge or witness, inspect the evidence before choosing a treatment. Compare breathing and pulse with what was reported.</p>
  <p><a href="../../metabolic-exposure/">Review the exposure evidence</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Cut, burn, loose debris, deformity or pain with movement</summary>
  <p>Use the exact site on the body chart. Open its covering if prompted. Check which finding changes the action and whether the chart also asks for <strong>Check circulation</strong> farther down the injured limb.</p>
  <p><a href="../../wounds/">Find a wound or limb action</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Soaked clothing, a weak pulse or cool hands after a major impact</summary>
  <p>Check each site, then repeat a current pressure and scan the affected region if the option is available. A dressed limb can coexist with a deeper injury.</p>
  <p><a href="../../major-trauma/">Compare bleeding and internal injuries</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Swelling, heat, or drainage around an implant</summary>
  <p>Examine the site and inspect the service port if offered. Check temperature, breathing and circulation for changes beyond the site.</p>
  <p><a href="../../recovery-specialist/#implant-findings">Implant findings</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Changed numbers after moving or reconnecting a patient</summary>
  <p>Look at the patient first. Compare chest rise and wrist pulse with the new sensor signal. Take a fresh cuff reading and check the time of the earlier record.</p>
  <p><a href="../../recovery-specialist/">Reassess during recovery</a></p>
</details>

</div>
