---
title: Fever, weakness, and other general symptoms
description: Fever, headache, confusion, weakness, abdominal pain, and temperature changes.
---

Ask when the problem began and what changed before or after it. Recheck the original complaint after treatment.

<div class="symptom-list">

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Cough, fever, chills, or a hot swollen wound</summary>
  <p>Use <strong>Take temperature</strong> where available, then look for a source. Compare both sides of the chest for cough, open the covering on a suspect wound, or ask about urination for flank pain. Trend pressure if circulation weakens.</p>
  <p><a href="../../infection/">Find the source and reassess</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Headache, dizziness or brief fainting</summary>
  <p>Ask about onset, what came just before the change and whether it has happened before. Check speech and movement at the head. A sudden peak can matter even when the first exam is reassuring.</p>
  <p><a href="../../neurologic/">Compare headache and fainting findings</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Slurred speech, uneven smile, arm drift or weaker grip</summary>
  <p>Ask when they were last acting normally. Compare both sides and <strong>Check glucose</strong> at a usable fingertip if fitted. Recheck the original weakness after correction. Keep a persistent deficit in the report even if the ECG looks ordinary.</p>
  <p><a href="../../neurologic/">Compare low glucose and stroke findings</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Vomiting, diarrhea, flank pain or lower belly pain</summary>
  <p>Ask where the pain started, whether it moved and whether they can drink. Select the abdomen. Compare diffuse cramping with a persistent tender spot. Mention flank pain and urinary symptoms together if both are present.</p>
  <p><a href="../../abdominal/">Compare abdominal findings</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Sweating, tremor, confusion, cramps or unusual temperature</summary>
  <p>Read the intake for missed meals, filtration or heat and cold. Choose <strong>Check glucose</strong>, <strong>Take temperature</strong> or <strong>Check chemistry</strong> according to that clue and the fitted kit.</p>
  <p><a href="../../metabolic-exposure/">Compare metabolic and temperature clues</a></p>
</details>

</div>
