---
title: Chest, breathing, and pulse symptoms
description: Chest pain, palpitations, a weak pulse, and breathing changes.
---

Start with the complaint and compare it with the patient’s chest movement or wrist pulse.

<div class="symptom-list">

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Chest burning, pressure, or stabbing pain</summary>
  <p>Ask when it started and whether meals, movement or breathing change it. Select the chest and compare focal tenderness, the two sides of the chest and any continuing pressure at rest. Use <strong>Acquire 12-lead</strong> when the recorder is fitted and symptoms suggest a coronary problem.</p>
  <p><a href="../../chest-pain/">Compare chest-pain causes</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Skipped, fast or slow beats, or a weak pulse</summary>
  <p>Use <strong>Check pulse</strong> at a usable wrist, attach the ECG at the chest and measure cuff BP at a usable upper arm. Compare rate and regularity with peripheral pulse and pressure before choosing a mode.</p>
  <p><a href="../../rhythms/">Compare rhythms and shock modes</a></p>
</details>

<details name="symptom" class="symptom-choice">
  <summary>Breathlessness, wheeze, hoarseness, weak chest rise</summary>
  <p>Check both sides of the chest and the mask or circuit if fitted. Ask about smoke, dust or another exposure. Compare a usable SpO₂ or EtCO₂ trace with actual movement. Uneven rise after impact also belongs under <a href="../../major-trauma/">Major trauma</a>.</p>
  <p><a href="../../breathing/">Compare breathing findings</a></p>
</details>

</div>
