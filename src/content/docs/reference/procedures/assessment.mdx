| Action | Where to find it and what it does |
| --- | --- |
| Ask about symptoms | At the counter or above the body chart. Use the available question about onset, pain, cough, exposure, or dizziness. During a follow-up, ask about first aid or repeat the site-specific check when offered. |
| Admit patient | Read the intake, available resources, and payer terms first. Accept at the window or use **Admit patient** on a prepared practice chart. The bedside clock starts when care begins. |
| Body exam and Exam findings | Use the bottom button. After 2.5 game seconds, read the report and select a body site for its current findings. Repeat after a change. The exam does not replace a monitor measurement or a witness account. |
| Examine, open a covering, or read a treatment card | Select the site named in the clue. Some charts let you look beneath clothing, open a boot, or read a treatment card at the bedside. Check the newly exposed site and re-examine if prompted. |
| Check pulse and Check circulation | Choose a usable hand or forearm for the timed pulse check. On injury charts, **Check circulation** may also appear on a hand or foot downstream of the affected site. A readable ECG and a felt pulse are separate findings. |
| Select diagnosis | Choose or revise the working diagnosis below the monitor. Use the history, exam, and tests you have. Many charts ask for a supported diagnosis before discharge or handover. Urgent treatment can start earlier. |
