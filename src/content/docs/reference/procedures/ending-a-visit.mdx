---
title: Observation, discharge, and handover
description: Complete observation, discharge or hand over the patient, and review the treatment record.
---

- **Discharge patient.** Complete the required care, diagnosis, observation and rechecks. The local discharge control turns green when the chart is ready. A minor case can finish without measurements the chart does not need.
- **Call Medbay and Hand over to Medbay.** Call on eligible visits that need further help. Keep the patient supported until the crew arrives. Finish the current handover checks, or use **Ask crew to take over** when the case offers that alternative. On a local-only visit, use the available bedside care and complete its observation.
- **Watch the change after treatment.** A new symptom or genuine deterioration interrupts the observation window. Treat the cause, confirm the relevant findings again, and allow a fresh watch to finish. If a signal drops out, check the probe or line and the patient before deciding what changed.
- **Check the observation countdown.** Some referred visits show a countdown above **Select diagnosis**. The header reads **ACTIVE CARE** during assisted breathing or while the patient is recovering. It changes to **UNDER OBSERVATION** when the countdown starts. If ECG monitoring drops out, check the leads and equipment. The countdown starts over once the ECG is back and the patient is stable. When it ends, make the fresh checks the chart asks for, then check the discharge control.
- **Complete specialist and post-operation checks.** In the available ward panel, request and file any required record, compare the marked observations, and finish the offered rehearsal before using specialist equipment. Once a procedure is running, verify pulse, breathing and the probe after a move. **Confirm transfer checks**, **File recovery check**, and **Sign completed observation** require the current findings shown beside them. If a fresh cuff expires, measure again. **Abort procedure** opens a confirmation when available.
- **Close consultations and visits.** Confirm any eligible deferred consultation, read the receipt, and return to the counter for the reply. Check the disposition before starting another appointment.
- **Open Case review or Recent cases.** Use the timeline filters, open a retained exam, and compare recorded strips and the timing of treatment. Read an authorized **Signed ward update** when it appears. At a follow-up visit, ask about changes and repeat the requested finding before signing or arranging more care.
