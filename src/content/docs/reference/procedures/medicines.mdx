Select the pictured **Medication tray** beside the bed or open **Syringe tray** from an eligible forearm. Confirm the exact lot, purity, and current price. Use **Pin Quick care action** for quick access to a medicine or dressing. A dose may require a syringe as well as the pack. A failed dose still uses the listed stock and can raise the next attempt’s response chance for that medicine on the same chart. Check the patient and the updated chance before retrying.

<WikiShot name="medicines-drawer-en" version="0.17.84" alt="Earlier Medicines drawer with forearm tabs and paging buttons beside the M4 monitor" caption="Earlier layout. The forearm controls choose where to administer the selected dose." />

For doses that require forearm access, choose **Left forearm** or **Right forearm** at the top of **Medicines**. For topical medicine, select the affected area directly on the patient. If a medicine has multiple lots, click its batch number, such as **Batch 1/2**, to cycle through them. If the button reads **Select batch**, choose the current lot before confirming. Use **More medicines** or **Previous medicines** at the bottom to change pages.

<WikiShot name="medicine-confirm-en" version="0.17.41" alt="GlucoRescue confirmation shows the selected forearm, quantity, purity, chance, and Confirm" caption="The full medicine name appears above the forearm controls. Check the side and lot, then select Confirm beside its quantity, purity, and response chance." />

| Action | What the chart checks |
| --- | --- |
| BroadGuard, GlucoRescue, and PulseLift | The corresponding infection finding, measured low glucose, or need for circulation support determines eligibility. Watch the response and repeat the relevant assessment before closing care. |
| ClearFlow infusion for a critical patient | Complete Body exam and obtain a current low blood pressure reading from the cuff or arterial line. Collect a bedside chemistry sample if the chart also asks for electrolytes. Fit and power the pump, open it from the ward, select a usable forearm, and choose **ClearFlow infusion**. Recheck blood pressure when the infusion ends. |
| ClearFlow for measured electrolyte imbalance | If the patient only needs electrolyte correction and the chemistry result supports it, select **Electrolyte support** and a lot at a usable forearm. Continue any needed breathing or circulation support while waiting for a response. |
| Naloxgone | Read the exposure record and confirm the indicated exposure in the chart. When the dose becomes available, check breathing again and continue observation. |
| Heparin | On eligible charts, find it at the forearm after the required coronary evidence. Confirm the dose before moving on to the chart’s next step. |
| Pressor Bridge | Grade III compound for eligible measured low pressure with a pulse. Use a forearm and recheck pressure and ECG during the support window. |
| Rhythm Buffer | Grade IV compound. The shortcut checks a pulse, recent pressure, readable ECG, and documented recurrence. A responding pack delays recurrence. Check the pressure again even if the tracing improves. |
| Epinephrine | Grade IV support for the chart’s documented absent pulse or sustained severe circulatory failure. When the forearm control enables, confirm the lot. Recheck circulation and keep required support running. |
| Native pain relief and local infection care | **Medicines** can offer monitored use of an Oxymore or Nomore Injector or an eligible Phagimycin Pill. Ask about symptoms, examine the patient, and attach the requested sensors. Reassess breathing and the treated site. |
| Native wound adjuncts and clinic biogel | Select the examined site to see whether the chart accepts salve, rubbing alcohol, roe, fish skin, or clinic-made biogel. These each have their own stock and eligibility. The same ward or recovery chart may offer sponsored Blue Blood. |
| Comfort, recovery plan, and follow-up medicine | Use **Offer PainEase**, **Discuss a recovery plan**, or other comfort actions only when offered. A monitored follow-up can allow Synaprest after the conversation. Some beds offer time with the patient or a crew takeover while you continue support. |
| Verified maintenance stock | When an eligible specialist chart offers protected stock, use the visible reserve or collection action. **Use verified Immunivax** checks the exact usable stock before recording the maintenance treatment. |
| Take-home prescription | Before a suitable discharge, select **Prescribe PainEase** or **Prescribe Phagimycin Pill** when offered. Review the price and confirm the stock before signing. |
