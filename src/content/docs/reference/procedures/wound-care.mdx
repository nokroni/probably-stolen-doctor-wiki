| Action | What to do in the game window |
| --- | --- |
| Hold pressure | At an exposed bleeding site, choose **Hold pressure** when available. Hold the pad inside the ring until it fills, then release the mouse button. The full ring stays visible, and the wound can still bleed. Pick an available dressing in the same window, wrap it, and recheck the site. |
| Clear loose debris | Select an examined surface wound and move the pictured loose pieces into the tray. This opens the next pressure or dressing action. A deep or fixed object uses a separate control. |
| Apply dressing or cover a burn | Select the affected site. Choose the shown dressing, burn cover, or eligible **Fish skin**. Follow the circular guide and release the mouse after the bar fills. Each completed wrap treats its selected site. Confirm the wound marker and recheck bleeding. |
| Apply splint | At a charted fracture, drag both pictured segments to their outlines and place the square plate over the join. Do one site at a time, then check circulation if the chart requests it. |
| Reduce joint | Open the joint panel from the examined site. Drag the rounded piece into its matching outline and release to lock it. Check the hand or other indicated distal site after the gesture. |
| Secure fragment | At the site of an embedded object, place the two pictured braces into their marked slots to stabilize it in the simulation. Reassess the wound and follow the chart’s transfer requirements. |
| Drain chest | Select the chest on an eligible Grade III or higher chart. Complete the indicated exam, ultrasound, pressure, and support checks and have a sterile procedure kit. Follow the window’s tool guide, then operate its lever. Recheck chest movement and pressure. Residual bleeding may need further care. |
| Automatic repair or Toolset prep | Select the documented source after the chart’s scan, pressure and support checks. Confirm the sterile kit and powered surgery core. Where offered, a compatible Surgery Toolset can assist an eligible surface revision. Keep the patient under observation and check the current result. |
| Capture and close with the surgery core | At an eligible scanned site, the chart checks circulation, recent pressure, blood support, and power. Fit the capture cassette in the game’s guides. Extraction and closure are separate commits. Repeat pressure and complete the seal or prepare the charted transfer. |
| Replant a finger | At the affected hand, **Check cassette and ask consent**. When the chart allows it, use the powered surgery core, microscope, and sterile finger coupler. Follow the cradle guides and validate with **Check flow at finger**. If the chart points to transfer, dress the hand and pass the recorded tissue cassette with the patient. |
| Major limb injury | Select the remaining forearm or lower leg to inspect the stump and control bleeding. Treat other wounds separately. If a hand is missing, put the finger probe on the other hand. Follow the bedside prompt when choosing an arm for the cuff or an infusion. Recheck pressure after dressings and review the tissue-custody note at handover. |
| Discuss, mark, or defer scar revision | Examine the area, discuss consent, and use **Mark scar outline** if the patient agrees. Respect an offered change to the outline or choose **Defer revision**. **Surface revision** requires the marked site, a current cuff reading, and a sterile kit. If the reusable toolset is in stock, the prepared cycle takes 8 seconds instead of 12. Check the patient after the carriage stops. Case Review records the completion separately from the outline. |
