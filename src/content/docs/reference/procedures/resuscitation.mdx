| Action | Where to find it and what to check |
| --- | --- |
| Support airway and Refit mask | Select the head or use the offered airway shortcut with the bag and mask fitted. Watch the bed for chest rise. If support becomes unreliable, follow the local **Refit mask** or **Reseat circuit** prompt and check again. |
| Start or stop ventilator | Use **Support** with an M4 and a powered ventilator. Check chest motion and the CO₂ trace when the sampling line is fitted. **Stop ventilator** stays in the bottom row when the device is running. |
| Manual CPR | Use **Start CPR** at the chest after the bedside confirms eligibility. Each tap of **Space** makes one in-game compression. The mouse remains available for other controls. **Stop CPR** stays at the bottom, including during defibrillator use. |
| Powered chest compressor | At the chest, open **Compressor**, choose **Fit compressor frame**, then **Start compressor** when ready. Check the power indicator and movement. AED analysis can pause powered compressions. The compressor can continue through eligible shocks. Stop it with the same bottom control. |
| Attach pads to chest | Select the chest, then move both numbered pads to the matching guides. Wait for the pads to connect, then select the pictured device by the bed. |
| AED analysis, charge, and deliver | In the AED window, use **Analyze rhythm** and read the advice. When it advises a shock and the controls permit it, use **Charge**, wait for readiness, and use **Deliver**. Manual compressions must stop during the blocked steps. Recheck the pulse and support after delivery. |
| Manual defib and SYNC | In the manual unit window, choose an available energy setting, charge, and follow the readiness indicators. Use **Enable SYNC** only when the chart supports synchronization and the monitor can show markers. Use **Disable SYNC** when the next intervention requires it. Treat **Review shock** as a warning to recheck the rhythm and pulse. Overriding it can hurt the simulated patient. |
| Start infusion | Use **Support** at a usable forearm with a powered infusion pump and compatible monitor. The chart checks the indicated ClearFlow stock and pressure findings. Read the trend while the infusion runs. |
| Screened blood and Blue Blood | Use the available blood-station action at an intact forearm. Select the correct bag and confirm the shown cost. Wound control or other definitive care can still be required. Recheck circulation and the pressure trend while support runs. |
| Start reperfusion | At the chest on an eligible Grade IV chart, review the current 12-lead and any prerequisite treatment. Confirm the action when the powered equipment is ready. Keep the ECG connected and check for recurrence or poor circulation during the new observation period. |
| Warm patient, Cool patient, or rest | **Support** offers warming or cooling when the findings support it. Some stable charts instead offer **Rest and reassess**, **Offer water and rest**, **Rest upright**, **Quiet rest**, or **Settle on the cot** at the bed. Watch the relevant finding drift over time and recheck. |
