---
title: "Monitoring and diagnostic tools"
description: "Connect bedside sensors, run tests, and compare fresh readings."
---

| Action | Where to find it and what to check |
| --- | --- |
| Attach ECG and Check connections | Select the chest with a powered monitor fitted. **Check connections** helps when an attached lead or probe is giving a poor signal. After a shock, let the sweep restore and compare the rhythm with a new pulse check. |
| Attach oximeter | Select an intact hand with a finger probe fitted and the monitor on. Wait for the pleth and SpO₂ readings to settle. If the chart reports a loose clip, **Refit finger clip** at the selected hand or under **Support**. Compare it with the wrist and the patient’s breathing. |
| Open pressure cuff, Wrap cuff, and Measure cuff BP | Select an eligible upper arm. Wrap the cuff in its panel, then start the reading. You can work on another problem while it cycles. A fresh measurement may be required after an intervention or a move. M4 offers **Auto cuff** and **Stop cuff cycle**. |
| Take temperature or Connect temperature probe | Open the thermometer at the head. A fitted handheld model issues a single reading. With a thermal monitor and powered M4, connect the probe for a continuous trend. **Disconnect probe** is in the bottom row. |
| Check glucose | Select a usable hand or choose **Diagnostics** with a fitted glucose sampler. Wait for the in-game reading. Repeat after treatment and compare any remaining grip or speech changes with the new finding. |
| Acquire 12-lead and Read twelve leads | Use the chest controls or **Instruments → Diagnostics**. Fit a recorder with a powered M3 or M4, connect the ECG, and leave the signal readable while it captures. Open the recorded printout to see the result and its age. |
| Save ECG | With a readable connected ECG on an M3 or M4, capture the current strip. Keep it for comparison after care or a recurrence. The 12-lead printout is recorded separately. |
| Connect / zero ART | Use a forearm or **Diagnostics** with a fitted transducer and compatible powered monitor. Wait for zeroing. When the channel is live, compare its ABP trend with the patient and the last cuff reading. |
| Capture ultrasound | In **Diagnostics**, select the chest, abdomen, or eligible limb site. Use the powered ultrasound and read the labeled result for that region. A scan of another site does not fulfill a site-specific requirement. |
| Check chemistry | Use a forearm or **Diagnostics** with the powered bedside lab. Complete the game’s collection gesture and dock the on-screen sample. Read the result before starting an indicated correction. |
| Fit, reconnect, or disconnect the CO₂ line | Use **Instruments → Support** with the powered M4 and ventilator fitted. Once attached, the channel can sample supported breaths. Wait for a complete breath and compare the wave with chest movement. Disconnecting blanks the channel. |
| Archive and flag pressure | **Archive** snapshots the current readings when the M4 and station controller have power. If a chart offers **Pressure holding** or **Flag low pressure**, collect the required readings and record what they show. |
