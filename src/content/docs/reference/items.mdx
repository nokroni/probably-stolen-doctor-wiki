The Doctor license unlocks normal equipment purchases. Grade I gets a starter rack. From Grade II, check the medical supplier’s rotating stock. A Medical Bay Supply Crate can contain equipment before its purchase grade, though advanced models appear less often. Fit the device to the rack and check its power and attachments before admitting a patient.

Keep supplies in unlocked owned storage. Move finished packs out of production machines before using them at the bed. The game shows the selected lot and current price before you confirm.
