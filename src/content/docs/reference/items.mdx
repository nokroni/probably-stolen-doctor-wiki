Item names follow the Doctor mod 0.17.40. All treatments and recipes below are **in-game mechanics**.

**Buying equipment.** Grade I issues the starter instruments. From Grade II, the medical supplier rotates eligible equipment and supplies at the counter. The grade in each table is the normal purchase grade. Medical Bay Supply Crates can contain equipment ahead of its normal purchase grade, though advanced models appear much less often. A recovered device still needs a compatible rack, power, and any companion equipment.

**Finding bedside stock.** Keep usable supplies in owned, unlocked shop storage, including the display case, back room, or Medicine cabinet. Move items out of a production machine before trying to use them at the bed. Confirmation shows the selected lot and what it costs at the current shop price. A confirmed use consumes the listed supplies even when the medicine has no effect.

| Recharger | Recharge a removed Energy Cell overnight using the original shop machine. Get replacements and upgrades through normal shop routes. |

## Clinic medicine and sterile supplies

The pharmacy rotates medicine lots, so compare their purity before buying. A new clinic has a starter BroadGuard pack, a starter GlucoRescue pack and sterile syringes. Fit the syringe tray and keep both packs and syringes in usable stock. The item's tooltip and dose confirmation show its purity. Recheck the patient before repeating a treatment.

## Farming and chemistry

Buy clinic seeds from the retired farmer after opening the Farmer route. Power a hydroponic unit, add water, fit a seed and let it grow overnight. Harvesting returns produce and a new seed. Use the native DNA manipulator and disks to move compatible traits to or from clinic crops. Seeds start with both a strength and a trade-off.

| Planting stock | Harvest | Default traits and main recipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pulsevine seeds | Pulsevine tips | Grows quickly and needs more water. Used for PulseLift extract. |
| Moonmoss spores | Moonmoss fronds | Hardy and lower-yielding. Used for BroadGuard extract. |
| Frostkelp spores | Frostkelp strips | Needs less water and is more sensitive to contaminants. Used for ClearFlow extract and sterile syringes. |
| Sugarpod seeds | Sugarpod kernels | Larger harvest and slower growth. Used for GlucoRescue and ClearFlow extracts. |

The clinic chemist recipes require both the Farmer and Chemist routes. Begin each batch with an empty graduated cylinder in the Blender. Check the vessel's tooltip after processing. Fit the prepared vessel in the Chemical Finisher, select the named extract and press **Output**. Each output uses 2 mL of the mixture. At the Sterile finishing bench, select the matching recipe to seal the finished extract.

| Finished extract | In-game processing route |
| --- | --- |
| PulseLift extract | Blend one Pulsevine tip, add 1 mL of the game's basic solution with a dropper, then heat on Low to the in-game reaction point of 55 °C. Add 2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer before heating to get a refined fraction. |
| BroadGuard extract | Blend one Moonmoss frond, add 1 mL of the game's acidic solution, then heat on Low to 45 °C. Add 2 mL of Sterile Clarifier before heating to get a refined fraction. |
| GlucoRescue extract | Blend one Sugarpod kernel and heat on Low to 65 °C. Pour the processed mixture through Kotton Fabric fitted in a clean Water Pitcher. Put the filtered pitcher in the finisher. Add 2 mL of Sterile Clarifier before heating to get a refined fraction. |
| ClearFlow extract | Blend one Frostkelp strip and one Sugarpod kernel. Fit the vessel in the powered Evaporator with a separate empty distillate cylinder and leave it overnight. Finish the dry residue from the original vessel. For the stabilized fraction, add 2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer and warm on Low to 40 °C. Transfer to the Evaporator when stabilized kelp salts appear. |

| Reagent bottle | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Bottle of Sterile Clarifier | Measure into eligible fictional crop mixtures. A full bottle is the carrier for Pressor Bridge and Epinephrine. |
| Bottle of Pulse Stabilizer | Measure into eligible fictional crop mixtures. A full bottle is the carrier for Rhythm Buffer. |

Open one owned **Common or Restricted Medical Supplies** pack to obtain a full 10 mL bottle of each reagent. Use a different, sealed pack for the cartridge recipe. Check the bottle before assembling a compound because the recipe needs its full contents.

### Compound recipes

| Finished pack | Grade | Fit in the Sterile finishing bench |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressor Bridge | III | One refined PulseLift extract, one stabilized ClearFlow extract and one full Bottle of Sterile Clarifier. |
| Rhythm Buffer | IV | One refined PulseLift extract, one refined BroadGuard extract, one full Bottle of Pulse Stabilizer and one Sterile cartridge. |
| Epinephrine | IV | One refined PulseLift extract, one refined GlucoRescue extract, one full Bottle of Sterile Clarifier and one Sterile cartridge. |

The bench checks both physical extracts and their assays. The compound takes the lower of the two clinic purity readings. Inspect the finished pack to see its input lots.
