---
title: "Clinic power"
description: "Fit native cells or a Current Converter and set up the shared Medical power hub."
---

Open a device from the equipment rack to reach its power slot. Fitted devices in the same rack draw from the Grade IV **Medical power hub** first. If the hub cannot supply a draw, the device tries its own power source.

Starting with 0.17.108, all four hub slots can hold Energy Cells. Charged cells split each draw evenly. If one runs out during a draw, the others split what remains. Any one slot can hold a Current Converter. When the cells lack enough combined charge for a draw, the hub tries the converter.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical power hub | IV | Fit the hub in the equipment rack. Load up to four Energy Cells, or use one of the slots for a Current Converter. |

| Base-game item | Clinic use |
| --- | --- |
| Energy Cell | Fits in a compatible electronic device or the Medical power hub. A new clinic gets two full 100-unit cells, one for the M2 and one for the AED. |
| Current Converter | Fits compatible devices and the Medical power hub. It must be switched on and loses power in a blackout. Daytime use costs Credits at the base-game rate. Nighttime use requires night power service. |
| Recharger | Recharge a removed Energy Cell overnight using the original shop machine. Get replacements and upgrades through normal shop routes. |
