---
title: "Clinic power"
description: "Fit native cells or a Current Converter and set up the shared Medical power hub."
---

Open a device from the equipment rack to reach its power slot. Keep a charged source available before starting measurements or treatment. The Grade IV hub accepts a compatible charged cell and powers multiple fitted devices. A Current Converter fits compatible individual devices, uses the native Credit rate, and loses power during grid outages.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical power hub | IV | Insert a charged compatible cell in the hub to supply fitted devices. Check the remaining power before starting a procedure. |

| Base-game item | Clinic use |
| --- | --- |
| Energy Cell | Fits in a compatible electronic device or the Medical power hub. A new clinic gets two full 100-unit cells, one for the M2 and one for the AED. |
| Current Converter | Fits compatible individual devices. Switch it on to draw shop power at the native rate. It needs available grid power and enough Credits. |
| Recharger | Recharge a removed Energy Cell overnight using the original shop machine. Get replacements and upgrades through normal shop routes. |
