1. Choose the patient's chest and open **Attach pads to chest**. Place both numbered pads on their matching guides.
2. With the AED, use **Analyze rhythm**. Follow its advice. The manual defibrillator lets you select the joules before charging.
3. Press **Charge**, wait for readiness, then choose **Deliver** if the chart and device permit it. Use **Stop CPR** at the bottom when manual compressions block an operation. A powered compressor may continue through an eligible shock.
4. After a shock, wait for the ECG display to resume, then reassess the rhythm and pulse separately.

Both units spend energy when charging. Without module bonuses, a 200 J charge takes 4 battery units and a 360 J charge takes 7.2. A charge that expires or is cleared still uses that energy.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portable AED | I | Analyzes the rhythm before advising a fixed 200 J shock. Comes with a full Energy Cell in the starter kit. |
| Manual defibrillator | II | Lets you select 100, 150, 200, 300, or 360 J. Has a native module bay. The game offers **Enable SYNC** for eligible SVT and AF with a pulse. VT and VF use unsynchronized shocks. |

An AED shock leaves a spike in one direction on the ECG. A manual shock leaves a spike in both directions. **RESTORING** marks the brief gap before the trace returns. If **Review shock** appears, recheck the rhythm and pulse. Confirming a shock for an unsuitable rhythm can cause loss of pulse.
