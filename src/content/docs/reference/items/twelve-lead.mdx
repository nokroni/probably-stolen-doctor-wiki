Fit the recorder and power the M3 or M4. At the chest, attach ECG and keep the signal readable. Choose **Acquire 12-lead** at the chest or under **Diagnostics**, then **Read twelve leads** to check the printout and its age. The monitor's **Save ECG** captures a separate short strip.

A fitted recorder can also be used on other visits. For example, a suspected stroke chart can return sinus rhythm with no regional ST elevation. Use the speech and movement findings to assess the original complaint.

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12-lead recorder | II | Records a diagnostic ECG after connecting chest leads. Uses power from the fitted M3 or M4. The result depends on the patient's findings. |
