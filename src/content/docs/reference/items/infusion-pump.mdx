title: "Infusion pump"
description: "Start and monitor eligible fluid support through a fitted line."
---

Fit and power the pump. Select the pictured infusion pump and a usable forearm, then choose **ClearFlow infusion**. Have the compatible monitor, a ClearFlow pack, and the pressure evidence requested by the chart. After starting the infusion, follow the current pressure and circulation findings.
