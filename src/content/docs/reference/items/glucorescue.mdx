---
title: "GlucoRescue"
description: "Use a glucose reading to guide the dose and recheck after the response."
---

Take a fingertip reading with a fitted Glucose sampler. When the current result supports GlucoRescue, select an eligible forearm, confirm the pack and syringe, and give the game dose. Glucose changes gradually. Take another reading before deciding whether treatment has worked.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GlucoRescue | Sealed glucose pack for a confirmed low reading. Recheck glucose as it changes. | Pharmacy or two packs from one GlucoRescue extract. |

| Finished extract | In-game processing route |
| --- | --- |
| GlucoRescue extract | Blend one Sugarpod kernel and heat on Low to 65 °C. Pour the processed mixture through Kotton Fabric fitted in a clean Water Pitcher. Put the filtered pitcher in the finisher. Add 2 mL of Sterile Clarifier before heating to get a refined fraction. |
