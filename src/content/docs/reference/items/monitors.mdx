1. Fit a monitor in the rack and connect a charged Energy Cell or an eligible power source.
2. At the chest, use **Attach ECG**. Connect the finger probe and cuff at their own body sites.
3. Read ECG and HR together, then the pulse waveform and SpO₂. NIBP is the last cuff reading. M4 also has separate channels for attached ABP and CO₂ equipment.
4. Use **Save ECG** on M3 or M4 with a readable signal. After a shock, wait for the sweep to return and check the pulse before drawing conclusions.

An unconnected sensor leaves its channel blank. Upgrade to M3 for strip memory and a 12-lead setup, or to M4 for automatic cuff cycles and advanced channels. The medical supplier brings upgrades from their purchase grade onward.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M2 bedside monitor | I | Starter monitor with live ECG, pulse oximetry and on-demand cuff pressure after connecting the appropriate leads and sensors. |
| M3 diagnostic monitor | II | Adds short ECG strip capture and memory to the basic monitoring channels. |
| M4 critical care monitor | III | Adds automatic cuff cycles and supports advanced channels from connected equipment, including ABP and EtCO₂. Accepts native modules. |
