---
title: "Sterile supplies"
description: "Match syringes, kits, cassettes, couplers, and cartridges to their own uses."
---

Read the stock line in the confirmation before you commit an operation. A syringe is bedside stock for sealed packs; a cartridge is an ingredient for the finishing bench. Capture and sealing may spend different supplies, so stock both if the chart lists them.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sterile syringe | Single-use item consumed with eligible sealed packs. | Starter stock, pharmacy, or two syringes from two Frostkelp strips at the bench. |
| Sterile procedure kit | Single-use sterile material for eligible drainage, external debris cleanup and repairs. | Rotating Grade III+ equipment supplier stock. |
| Sealed capture cassette | One disposable insert for an eligible retained-object capture using the surgery core. | Rotating Grade IV equipment supplier stock. |
| Sterile finger coupler | One disposable fitting used with the Microscope cradle. | Rotating Grade IV equipment supplier stock. |
| Sterile cartridge | Recipe material for Rhythm Buffer or Epinephrine. | At the Grade IV bench, turn one sealed Common or Restricted Medical Supplies pack into two cartridges. |
