---
title: "Sterile supplies"
description: "Match syringes, kits, cassettes, couplers, and cartridges to their own uses."
---

Read the stock line in the confirmation before you commit an operation. A syringe is bedside stock for sealed packs; a cartridge is an ingredient for the finishing bench. Capture and sealing may spend different supplies, so stock both if the chart lists them.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sterile syringe | Single-use item consumed with eligible sealed packs. | Starter stock, pharmacy, or two syringes from two Frostkelp strips at the bench. |
| Sterile procedure kit | Single-use sterile material for eligible drainage, external debris cleanup and repairs. | Rotating Grade III+ equipment supplier stock. |
| Sealed capture cassette | One disposable insert for an eligible retained-object capture using the surgery core. | Rotating Grade IV equipment supplier stock. |
| Sterile finger coupler | One disposable fitting used with the Microscope cradle. | Rotating Grade IV equipment supplier stock. |
| Sterile cartridge | Recipe material for Rhythm Buffer or Epinephrine. | With a Grade IV license and both the Farmer and Chemist routes open, turn one sealed Common or Restricted Medical Supplies pack into two cartridges at the bench. |

## Crafting sterile supplies

| Bench recipe | Ingredients | Yield | Requirements |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Sterile syringe | 2 [Frostkelp strips](../clinic-crops/) | 2 syringes | Obtain the Sterile finishing bench and two harvest items. Load the strips straight into the bench. |
| Sterile cartridge | 1 sealed pack of Common or Restricted Medical Supplies | 2 cartridges | Grade IV license and both the Farmer and Chemist routes. Load the unopened pack into the input grid. |

At the **Sterile finishing bench**, select **Choose recipe** and the matching recipe. Load the input, select **Craft**, and collect both pieces from the output on the right. For a compound, move a cartridge back into the input grid. If you need reagent bottles too, double-click a separate pack of Medical Supplies. [Clinic reagent bottles](../clinic-reagents/)
