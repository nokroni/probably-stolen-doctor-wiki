---
title: "Bedside equipment rack"
description: "Fit, replace, and power the equipment the bedside can use."
---

Select **Open equipment rack** at the empty bed. The header shows the fitted count. Hover over a mounted device to check its power, or open it to reach its power socket or supported module bay. Fit devices in the rack to use them at the bedside. When finished, select **Return to bedside**.

## Items and sources

| Item | Use and source |
| --- | --- |
| Bedside equipment rack | Takes **4 × 6** shelf cells. Its **18 × 12** interior holds the complete Grade IV equipment set. Comes with the starter kit. A supplier can replace a missing rack. |
