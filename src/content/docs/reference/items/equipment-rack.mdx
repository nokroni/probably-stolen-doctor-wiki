---
title: "Bedside equipment rack"
description: "Fit, replace, and power the equipment the bedside can use."
---

Select **Open equipment rack** at the empty bed. Move a device into the rack, then open the device itself to reach its power socket or supported module bay. A device left on the shop shelf won't unlock its bedside procedure.

## Items and sources

| Item | Use and source |
| --- | --- |
| Bedside equipment rack | Opens the actual inventory where clinic instruments are fitted. Comes with the starter kit. A supplier can replace a missing rack. |
