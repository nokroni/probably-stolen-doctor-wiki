The bedside action needs a documented absent pulse or sustained severe circulatory failure on an eligible visit. Complete the check the chart asks for, then choose an eligible forearm and confirm the selected stock. Maintain the indicated support and recheck circulation. A dose may already be active, so read the disabled reason before trying to repeat it.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Epinephrine | Grade IV rescue pack for short-term support during severe circulatory failure. | Grade IV sellers or the compound recipe below. |

## Crafting recipe

**Requires a Grade IV license and both the Farmer and Chemist routes.** This fictional in-game recipe produces **1 pack**.

| Bench ingredient | Quantity | Preparation and checks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Refined Pulsevine fraction | 1 extract, 2 mL | Make [PulseLift extract](../pulselift/#crafting-recipe). Add **2 mL Pulse Stabilizer** before heating the Pulsevine. Look for Refined Pulsevine in the finished extract. |
| Refined Sugarpod fraction | 1 extract, 2 mL | Make [GlucoRescue extract](../glucorescue/#crafting-recipe). Add **2 mL Sterile Clarifier** before heating the Sugarpod. After heating, filter through Kotton Fabric and run the filtrate through the finisher. Look for Refined Sugarpod in the extract. |
| [Bottle of Sterile Clarifier](../clinic-reagents/) | 1 full bottle, 10 mL | Set aside a full bottle for the bench. Its total volume must be 10 mL and all of it must be Sterile Clarifier. |
| [Sterile cartridge](../sterile-supplies/#crafting-sterile-supplies) | 1 cartridge | Keep a pack of Medical Supplies sealed. The **Sterile cartridge** recipe turns it into two cartridges. |

1. Make each extract in the **Chem Finisher** and save it for the compound. Check for the refined ingredients in the spectrometer, then look for the sealing confirmation and clinic purity in each extract’s details.
2. Open the **Sterile finishing bench**, select **Choose recipe → Epinephrine**, and load all four items. Select **Craft**.
3. Collect the pack. Its purity is the lower of the two clinic assays.

While preparing ingredients, draw the 2 mL for Sugarpod from a separate bottle and save a full one for the bench. If **Craft** is disabled, check the cartridge, the carrier’s contents and volume, and whether the Sugarpod went through filtering and finishing.
