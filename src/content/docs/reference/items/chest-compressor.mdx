---
title: "Chest compressor"
description: "Fit the compressor frame and keep powered compressions running when appropriate."
---

At the chest, choose **Compressor → Fit compressor frame → Start compressor** when ready. Inspect the power indicator and the frame's motion. AED analysis can pause the device, while eligible shocks can be delivered with it running. **Stop CPR** at the bottom stops the current compression action.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chest compressor | III | Runs powered chest compressions when fitted at the chest. It can continue during the game's shock delivery. Keep power available. |
