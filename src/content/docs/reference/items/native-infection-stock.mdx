---
title: "Phagimycin and native infection stock"
description: "Distinguish licensed clinical stock from the unlicensed native trade item."
---

Read the actual label when buying or selling. From Grade II, licensed Phagimycin can appear under **Medicines** after you examine and dress an eligible stable infection. Reassess after giving it. A chart can also offer a separate take-home prescription at discharge. The unlicensed tablet remains an original trading item with its own provenance. BroadGuard is stocked separately.

## Items and sources

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Phagimycin Pill | Eligible take-home prescription or supervised care for selected stable infections. A pill is included in the starting supplies. |
| Unlicensed Phagimycin Pill | Native counter trade. Provenance can matter during the offer, and licensed stock is the one used in bedside confirmation. |
