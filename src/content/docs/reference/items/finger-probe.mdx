---
title: "Finger pulse probe"
description: "Connect the finger clip and read pulse oximetry on a powered monitor."
---

This probe comes with the starting rack. Select an intact hand and choose **Attach oximeter**. Wait for the pleth and SpO₂ to settle. If the clip becomes unreliable, **Refit finger clip** at the hand or under **Support**, then compare the reading with the pulse check and the patient's breathing.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finger pulse probe | I | Attach to a finger to display the pulse and SpO₂ on the powered monitor. The pleth waveform represents the detected pulse. |
