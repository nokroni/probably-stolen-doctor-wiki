Fit and power the core. At the examined site, follow the chart's readiness checks and choose the offered repair, capture, or seal action. Capture and sealing can be separate steps. Leave enough power and keep a compatible sterile kit in stock for the next operation. The native Surgery Toolset is reusable and supports only the procedures that list it.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automatic surgery core | III | Powers eligible capture, repair and sealing procedures. Each operation lists its own disposable supplies. |

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Surgery Toolset | Reusable equipment for eligible advanced cleanup or scar work. Keep it in stock with the appropriate disposable materials. |
