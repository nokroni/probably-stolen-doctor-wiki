---
title: "Wound adjuncts and fish supplies"
description: "Use topical stock, prepared grafts, and eligible unfertilized roe at an examined site."
---

For an eligible shallow injury, choose the examined site and look under **Medicines** or the local care controls. Check the exact lot before applying a salve, prep, or graft. These have separate eligibility from dressings. Prepared tilapia stock comes from the original aquaculture route. Keep fertilized roe for breeding and use unfertilized roe only when the bedside lists it.

## Items and sources

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Nightingale's Salve | Comfort care for an examined shallow scrape. |
| Rubbing Alcohol | Optional external prep for eligible superficial wounds. |
| Tilapia Skin Graft | Native fish-supply item for eligible wound care and a Grade IV Clinic Biogel ingredient. |
| Graft Tilapia Roe | Unfertilized roe can help an examined scrape from Grade III. Fertilized roe remains breeding stock. |

After applying Nightingale’s Salve, run another **Body exam** and look up the treated area in **Exam findings**. A patient who can answer will tell you the scrape stings less. If it’s still bleeding, continue wound care and apply a dressing.
