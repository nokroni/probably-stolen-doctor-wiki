---
title: "Clinic crops and genetics"
description: "Compare the four clinic crops, their seeds, harvests, and native DNA traits."
---

Unlock the Farmer route in Wild's Network or start as a Farmer. Buy the seed or spores from the retired farmer. Power the Hydroponic System, add water, and insert one planting item. Growth advances overnight. The harvest returns produce and a seed. Use the native DNA tools for compatible trait extraction, transfer or removal.

## Items and sources

| Planting stock | Harvest | Default traits and main recipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pulsevine seeds | Pulsevine tips | Grows quickly and needs more water. Used for PulseLift extract. |
| Moonmoss spores | Moonmoss fronds | Hardy and lower-yielding. Used for BroadGuard extract. |
| Frostkelp spores | Frostkelp strips | Needs less water and is more sensitive to contaminants. Used for ClearFlow extract and sterile syringes. |
| Sugarpod seeds | Sugarpod kernels | Larger harvest and slower growth. Used for GlucoRescue and ClearFlow extracts. |

| Base-game item | Clinic crafting use |
| --- | --- |
| Hydroponic System | Grows clinic crops overnight with a powered, watered setup. |
| DNA Manipulator, DNA Data Disk and Genetic Stabilizer Module | Extract and apply compatible crop traits. Use the stabilizer module when you want to keep the donor while removing a trait rank. |
