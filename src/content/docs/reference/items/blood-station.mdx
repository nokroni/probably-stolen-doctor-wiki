---
title: "Blood station and blood bags"
description: "Match the bedside action to the correct blood product and check the pressure response."
---

Fit and power the Blood station, and keep the sealed bag in eligible owned stock. Select a usable forearm and choose **Screened blood** when the chart accepts a regular Blood Bag. The **Blue Blood** action appears separately when the findings support it. Check the selected item and its price before confirming, then recheck circulation and carry on with any remaining treatment.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blood station | III | Checks and delivers a compatible regular Blood Bag. Fit a power source and keep sealed bags in eligible stock. |

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Blood Bag | Ordinary transfusion stock. Grade III+ pharmacy visitors can bring it, including an additional bag when stock runs low. |
| Bag of “Blue Blood” | Vascular support for assessed eligible conditions. It has a separate action from ordinary transfusion and can appear with Grade IV suppliers. |
