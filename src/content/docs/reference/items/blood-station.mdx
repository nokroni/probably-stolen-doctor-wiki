Fit and power the Blood station, and keep the sealed bag in eligible owned stock. Select the pictured blood station and a usable forearm, then choose **Screened blood** when the chart accepts a regular Blood Bag. The **Blue Blood** action appears separately when the findings support it. Check the selected item and its price before confirming, then recheck circulation and carry on with any remaining treatment.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blood station | III | Fit and power the station for either regular transfusion or Blue Blood. Each action uses its own sealed bag. |

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Blood Bag | Ordinary transfusion stock. Grade III+ pharmacy visitors can bring it, including an additional bag when stock runs low. |
| Bag of “Blue Blood” | Vascular support for assessed eligible conditions. It has a separate action from ordinary transfusion and can appear with Grade IV suppliers. |
