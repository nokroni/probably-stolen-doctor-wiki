---
title: "Bedside lab"
description: "Collect a bedside sample and read the game’s chemistry result."
---

Fit and power the analyzer. From an eligible forearm or **Diagnostics**, choose **Check chemistry**. Follow the visual collection guide and dock the sample in the analyzer. Read the result before choosing any corrective treatment.
