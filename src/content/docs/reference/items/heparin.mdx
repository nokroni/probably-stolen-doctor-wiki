At an eligible forearm, choose Heparin once the chart has the required coronary evidence. Confirm the pack and any listed syringe. Check that the treatment was recorded before proceeding to the next coronary action.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heparin | Anticoagulant pack for eligible coronary care. | Grade III pharmacy stock or the Grade IV crafting recipe below. |

## Crafting recipe

**Requires a Grade IV license and both the Farmer and Chemist routes.** This fictional in-game recipe produces **1 pack**.

| Bench ingredient | Quantity | Preparation and checks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [ClearFlow](../clearflow/#crafting-recipe) | 1 pack | Use a sealed pack. Any purity qualifies, and the Heparin inherits this lot's purity up to 99%. |
| [PulseLift](../pulselift/#crafting-recipe) | 1 pack | Use a sealed pack at **95% purity or higher**. Prepare the refined extract with Pulse Stabilizer, seal it, and check its purity. |

At the **Sterile finishing bench**, select **Choose recipe → Heparin**. Load both sealed packs, select **Craft**, and collect the result. If **Craft** is disabled, check PulseLift purity and the crafting requirements. For high-purity Heparin, use a high-purity ClearFlow pack as well.

### Worked example

In the 0.17.109 test, **one sealed 88% ClearFlow pack** and **one sealed 99% PulseLift pack** produced **one 88% Heparin pack**. The PulseLift cleared the 95% requirement, while the ClearFlow set the result. Check the purity on each sealed pack before putting it in the bench.
