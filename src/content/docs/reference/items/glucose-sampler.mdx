A starter rack includes the sampler. Choose a usable hand or open **Diagnostics**, then select **Check glucose**. The bedside uses the result to decide whether a glucose treatment is available. Recheck after treatment. If a grip or speech finding persists, reassess it separately.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glucose sampler | I | Fit to the rack, then use a fingertip to get a glucose reading. Part of the starter kit. |
