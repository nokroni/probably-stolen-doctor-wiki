---
title: "Pressor Bridge"
description: "Use a Grade III compound for eligible low pressure and recheck the response."
---

Check that there is a pulse and take the fresh pressure the chart requests. When the forearm action becomes available, confirm the selected lot and syringe. Recheck pressure and ECG while the bridge is active. The temporary support can give you time to address the cause shown by the chart.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressor Bridge | Grade III compound for temporary pressure support. Recheck pressure and rhythm. | Grade III sellers or the compound recipe below. |

## Crafting recipe

**Requires a Grade III license and both the Farmer and Chemist routes.** One craft produces **1 pack**.

| Bench ingredient | Quantity | Preparation and checks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Refined Pulsevine fraction | 1 extract, 2 mL | Make [PulseLift extract](../pulselift/#crafting-recipe). Add **2 mL Pulse Stabilizer** before heating the Pulsevine. Look for Refined Pulsevine in the finished extract. |
| Stabilized Frostkelp fraction | 1 extract, 2 mL | Make [ClearFlow extract](../clearflow/#crafting-recipe). Add **2 mL Pulse Stabilizer** to the mixed Frostkelp and Sugarpod sap. Form the salts at **40–64 °C**, dry the mixture and run it through the finisher. Look for Stabilized kelp salts in the extract. |
| [Bottle of Sterile Clarifier](../clinic-reagents/) | 1 full bottle, 10 mL | Open Medical Supplies for a fresh bottle. Its total volume must be 10 mL and all of it must be Sterile Clarifier. |

1. Use the **Chem Finisher** to make the two extracts separately. Save both extracts for this recipe. Check their composition in the spectrometer and look for the sealing confirmation and purity in each item’s details.
2. Open the **Sterile finishing bench**, select **Choose recipe → Pressor Bridge**, and load all three items. Select **Craft** and collect the pack.
3. Read the output’s details. The finished pack takes the **lower clinic assay** of the two extracts and lists the input lots. A 96% extract and a 92% extract yield a 92% pack.

If **Craft** is disabled, check that both extracts contain their refined ingredients and that the carrier bottle is still full. ClearFlow extract also has to pass the dry check.
