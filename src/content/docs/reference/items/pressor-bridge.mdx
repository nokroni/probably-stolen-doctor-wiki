---
title: "Pressor Bridge"
description: "Use a Grade III compound for eligible low pressure and recheck the response."
---

Check that there is a pulse and take the fresh pressure the chart requests. When the forearm action becomes available, confirm the selected lot and syringe. Recheck pressure and ECG while the bridge is active. The temporary support can give you time to address the cause shown by the chart.
