---
title: "Rhythm Buffer"
description: "Check the recorded rhythm and pressure before giving this Grade IV compound."
---

In an eligible visit, document the recurrence, check for a pulse, and get a recent pressure and readable ECG. At an eligible forearm, confirm the selected lot and syringe. A responding dose can buy more time between rhythm episodes but can also lower pressure. Record a fresh pressure and continue the chart's monitoring.

## Crafting recipe

**Requires a Grade IV license and both the Farmer and Chemist routes.** One craft produces **1 pack**.

| Bench ingredient | Quantity | Preparation and checks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Refined Pulsevine fraction | 1 extract, 2 mL | Make [PulseLift extract](../pulselift/#crafting-recipe). Add **2 mL Pulse Stabilizer per tip** before heating. For several tips, use the [larger-batch example](../pulselift/#making-a-larger-batch). Look for Refined Pulsevine in the finished extract. |
| Refined Moonmoss fraction | 1 extract, 2 mL | Make [BroadGuard extract](../broadguard/#crafting-recipe). Use **2 mL Sterile Clarifier per frond** before heating. Look for Refined Moonmoss in the finished extract. |
| [Bottle of Pulse Stabilizer](../clinic-reagents/) | 1 full bottle, 10 mL | Set aside a full bottle for the bench. Its total volume must be 10 mL and all of it must be Pulse Stabilizer. |
| [Sterile cartridge](../sterile-supplies/#crafting-sterile-supplies) | 1 cartridge | Keep a pack of Medical Supplies sealed. The **Sterile cartridge** recipe turns it into two cartridges. |

1. Make each extract in the **Chem Finisher** and save it for the compound. Check for the refined ingredients in the spectrometer, then look for the sealing confirmation and clinic purity in each extract’s details.
2. Open the **Sterile finishing bench**, select **Choose recipe → Rhythm Buffer**, and load all four items. Select **Craft**.
3. Collect the pack and inspect it. The finished purity is the lower of the two clinic assays shown for the input lots.

Draw the stabilizer for processing Pulsevine from a separate bottle. If **Craft** is disabled, check the cartridge slot, the carrier’s contents and volume, and the refined ingredients in both extracts.

### Worked example

In the 0.17.109 test, the bench took one **99% PulseLift extract** containing Refined Pulsevine, one **99% BroadGuard extract** containing Refined Moonmoss, one **full 10 mL Pulse Stabilizer bottle**, and **one Sterile cartridge**. Check the bottle separately from the two 2 mL extracts. A bottle with 8 mL left after measuring reagents fails the bench check. **Craft** produced **one 99% Rhythm Buffer pack**.
