Fit the starter tray. At a usable forearm, open **Syringe tray** or choose the offered action under **Medicines**. Check the lot, purity, ingredients and price. You can also open **Instruments → Treatment tray** or use a pinned Quick Care action. When you confirm a sealed pack, stock is spent even if the patient does not respond.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Syringe tray | I | Prepares and confirms a sealed medication dose. Fit it to the rack and select a usable forearm. The tray shows the lot, purity and supplies required. |
