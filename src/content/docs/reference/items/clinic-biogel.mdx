---

When the chart offers gel care, confirm the treatment at the relevant site and complete the follow-up checks shown in the visit.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinic Biogel | Adjunct for eligible scheduled vascular recovery. | At Grade IV, craft it at the bench or look for it on the relief supply order. |

## Crafting recipe

**Requires a Grade IV license and both the Farmer and Chemist routes.** One craft produces **1 Clinic Biogel**.

| Bench ingredient | Quantity | Preparation and checks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Tilapia Skin Graft](../wound-adjuncts/) | 1 graft | Use the prepared graft from the base game's aquaculture route. |
| [PulseLift](../pulselift/#crafting-recipe) | 1 pack | Use the sealed medicine pack. Its details must show **90% purity or higher**. |

At the **Sterile finishing bench**, select **Choose recipe → Clinic Biogel**. Load both ingredients, select **Craft**, and move the finished gel into accessible shop stock. If **Craft** is disabled, check the graft, the sealed pack's purity, and your clinic grade.
