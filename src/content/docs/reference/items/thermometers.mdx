---
title: "Thermometers and thermal monitor"
description: "Compare handheld temperature checks and the M4’s continuous temperature channel."
---

Select the head. Use **Take temperature** with a fitted mercury or digital thermometer. The Grade I mercury model is part of the starter kit. Digital is faster and sold from Grade III. With the Grade IV thermal device and a powered M4, use **Connect temperature probe** for a continuous reading. The bottom row has **Disconnect probe**.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercury thermometer | I | Select the head for a manual temperature check. The reading takes a few seconds. |
| Digital thermometer | III | Takes a faster handheld temperature reading at the head. |
| Thermal monitor | IV | Attach its temperature probe at the head. A powered M4 shows the continuous reading. |
