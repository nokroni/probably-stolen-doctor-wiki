Read the chart's circulation findings and measure pressure when prompted. At an eligible forearm, choose PulseLift in **Medicines** or the syringe tray. Watch the response and continue other indicated treatment. High-purity sealed packs are also inputs for PainEase, Clinic Biogel and Heparin. Compounds use the refined extract itself.

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PulseLift | Sealed circulation-support pack. Watch pressure and follow the patient’s response. | Pharmacy or two packs from one PulseLift extract. |

## Crafting recipe

**These amounts are for one Pulsevine tip.** Use 1 mL from the blue Bottle of Basic Solution. For a refined batch, add **2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer per tip**.

1. Blend the Pulsevine tip into a clean 25 mL graduated cylinder to collect **Pulsevine sap**.
2. For one tip, add one full 1 mL draw from the blue bottle. For a refined batch, also add two full draws of Pulse Stabilizer before heating. Use the amounts below when blending several tips together.
3. Fit the cylinder on the Heating Plate and select **Low**. Once the actual temperature reaches **55 °C**, check for **Activated Pulsevine**. A refined batch also shows **Refined Pulsevine**.
4. Fit the cylinder in the [Chem Finisher](../chemistry-tools/) and select **PulseLift extract**. Set the quantity slider and click **Output**. Each extract uses 2 mL of mixture.
5. One accepted extract makes **2 PulseLift packs** at the [sterile finishing bench](../sterile-bench/). To make [Pressor Bridge](../pressor-bridge/), [Rhythm Buffer](../rhythm-buffer/), or [Epinephrine](../epinephrine/), use the extract containing Refined Pulsevine directly.
6. Check the tooltip on the sealed pack if you want to craft with it. Use a pack with **at least 90%** purity for [PainEase](../painease/) or [Clinic Biogel](../clinic-biogel/), or **at least 95%** for [Heparin](../heparin/).

### Single-tip test

In 0.17.109, one tip, 1 mL of blue-bottle solution and 2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer produced **three accepted extracts at 99%** with a stock Blender and Chem Finisher. At **55 °C** or above, check for both Activated Pulsevine and Refined Pulsevine before finishing. Sealing all three extracts would give six packs. The same one-tip recipe without stabilizer yielded **two accepted extracts at 64%**, enough for four packs.

### Making a larger batch

- **Measure Pulse Stabilizer per tip.** For example, collect the sap from five tips in a 50 mL cylinder and add **10 mL of Pulse Stabilizer** before heating. The amount refined depends on the stabilizer added, up to **80% of the raw sap volume**. If your yield is low, excess stabilizer stays in the mix.
- **Measure Base against the actual sap volume.** Read the **Pulsevine sap** row in the vessel tooltip after blending. The reaction needs at least **1 mL of Base for every 5 mL of raw sap**. If those five tips yield **18 mL of sap**, add blue-bottle solution until the **Base** row shows at least **3.6 mL**. Blender upgrades can change the sap yield.
- **Add both liquids before heating.** Once the raw sap has activated, adding more stabilizer cannot increase the refined fraction in that batch.
- **Set the number of extracts at the finisher.** One click on **Output** can make several extracts. The **2 mL** shown in the finisher is the amount of prepared mixture used for each extract. The amount of mixture and the available output space limit how many it makes.
