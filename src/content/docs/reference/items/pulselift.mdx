Read the chart's circulation findings and measure pressure when prompted. At an eligible forearm, choose PulseLift in **Medicines** or the syringe tray. Watch the response and continue other indicated treatment. High-purity sealed packs are also inputs for PainEase, Clinic Biogel and Heparin. Compounds use the refined extract itself.

## Crafting recipe

**Ingredients**　1 Pulsevine tip and 1 mL from the blue Bottle of Basic Solution. For a refined extract for compound crafting, also prepare 2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer.

1. Blend the Pulsevine tip into a clean 25 mL graduated cylinder to collect **Pulsevine sap**.
2. Draw 1 mL from the blue bottle with a 1 mL Dropper and add it to the cylinder. For a refined batch, add two full draws of Pulse Stabilizer before heating.
3. Fit the cylinder on the Heating Plate and select **Low**. Once the actual temperature reaches **55 °C**, check for **Activated Pulsevine**. A refined batch also shows **Refined Pulsevine**.
4. Fit the cylinder in the [Chem Finisher](../chemistry-tools/), select **PulseLift extract**, and output an extract. Each output uses 2 mL.
5. One accepted extract makes **2 PulseLift packs** at the [sterile finishing bench](../sterile-bench/). To make [Pressor Bridge](../pressor-bridge/), [Rhythm Buffer](../rhythm-buffer/), or [Epinephrine](../epinephrine/), use the extract containing Refined Pulsevine directly.
6. Check the tooltip on the sealed pack if you want to craft with it. Use a pack with **at least 90%** purity for [PainEase](../painease/) or [Clinic Biogel](../clinic-biogel/), or **at least 95%** for [Heparin](../heparin/).
