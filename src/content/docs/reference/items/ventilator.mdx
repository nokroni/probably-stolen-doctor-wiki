---
title: "Transport ventilator"
description: "Use powered ventilation and connect the M4’s exhaled CO₂ channel."
---

With a fitted M4 and powered ventilator, use **Support** to start ventilation. To view EtCO₂, fit the CO₂ line in **Instruments → Support** and wait a full sampled breath. Compare the trace with chest movement. If the chart says the circuit has slipped, use **Reseat circuit**. **Stop ventilator** remains at the bottom while running.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transport ventilator | III | Supports breathing while fitted and powered. The M4 can display the sampled EtCO₂ trace. |
