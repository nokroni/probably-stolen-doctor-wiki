Fit a powered ventilator and M4. Select the pictured ventilator and use **Fit CO₂ line** to sample spontaneous breathing with the ventilator stopped. Wait for a full breath, then compare the EtCO₂ trace and measured breathing rate with chest movement.

When the patient needs breathing support, **Start ventilator** also connects the sampling line. If the chest stops rising as expected, check the mask or use **Reseat circuit** as prompted. **Stop ventilator** stays at the bottom while the machine runs. A line you connected separately can keep sampling once ventilation stops. Disconnecting it blanks the EtCO₂ channel.
