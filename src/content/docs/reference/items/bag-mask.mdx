---
title: "Bag and mask"
description: "Fit the starter mask and check whether breathing support remains effective."
---

At the head, choose **Support airway** with the bag and mask fitted in the rack. Watch for chest movement. If the bedside reports a leak or poor fit, use **Refit mask** and recheck. A functioning ECG does not tell you whether the chest is moving.
