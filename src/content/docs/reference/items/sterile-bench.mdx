---

Open the bench and choose **Choose recipe**. Put the exact listed items into the material slots. An extract comes from the native Chem Finisher. Crafting checks its preparation and assay. For standard packs, one eligible extract makes two sealed packs and carries forward its assay. For a compound, fit both refined extracts, a full carrier bottle and any named seal. The finished compound takes the lower of the two clinic purity readings and displays the input lots on its tooltip.

If **Craft** stays disabled, compare every ingredient and required clinic level with the selected recipe. Move finished stock to shop storage so it can be used at the bed.

## Items and sources

| Item | Use and source |
| --- | --- |
| Sterile finishing bench | Seals extracts, makes sterile supplies and combines specialty packs. Look for it through the retired chemist once both the Farmer and Chemist routes are unlocked. It can also appear on the equipment supplier's cart. |
