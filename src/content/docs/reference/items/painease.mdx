When the chart offers **Give PainEase**, check the selected tablet and the price. Some visits offer it as a take-home prescription before discharge.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PainEase | Comfort tablet for eligible injury care or a take-home prescription. | Pharmacy or one sealed PulseLift pack of at least 90% purity at the bench. |

## Crafting recipe

**Requires both the Farmer and Chemist routes.** One sealed [PulseLift pack](../pulselift/#crafting-recipe) at **90% purity or higher** makes **1 PainEase tablet**.

1. Follow the PulseLift recipe to make the extract, then seal it at the Sterile finishing bench. Keep one sealed pack and check its purity in the item details.
2. At the **Sterile finishing bench**, select **Choose recipe → PainEase**. Load the pack, select **Craft**, and collect the tablet from the output slot.

If the ingredient slot shows an unmet requirement, check the item name, the pack’s purity, and the two unlocks.

### Worked example

Seal one accepted **99% PulseLift extract** into two packs. Move **one** pack into the left grid and leave the other in stock. Select **PainEase** and click **Craft**. The 0.17.109 test produced **1 PainEase tablet** in the right slot. Keep lower-purity packs for bedside care and use a pack of **90% or higher** for the recipe.
