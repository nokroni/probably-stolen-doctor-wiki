title: "PainEase"
description: "Use a sealed tablet for eligible comfort care or an offered take-home prescription."

When the chart offers **Give PainEase**, check the selected tablet and the price. Some visits instead offer it as a take-home prescription before discharge. If you craft it, put the eligible high-purity sealed PulseLift pack in the Sterile finishing bench and choose **PainEase**.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PainEase | Comfort tablet for eligible injury care or a take-home prescription. | Pharmacy or one sealed PulseLift pack of at least 90% purity at the bench. |
