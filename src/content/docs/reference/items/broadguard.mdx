Keep the sealed pack and a sterile syringe in accessible stock. Check the infection findings first, then open **Medicines** or the tray at a usable forearm when the chart allows the pack. After the dose, watch for a response and repeat the relevant check. Fever alone is one finding and can have several causes.

After **Body exam**, you can **Call Medbay** if a critical patient still needs infection treatment and you are out of usable doses. This includes running out of packs or syringes, or using the last dose without a response. Continue the required support, observation, and rechecks, then select a working diagnosis before handover. The handover record says the infection still needs treatment. See [Observation, discharge, and handover](../../procedures/ending-a-visit/).

## Crafting recipe

**Ingredients**　1 Moonmoss frond and 1 mL from the red Bottle of Acidic Solution. For a refined extract for compound crafting, also prepare 2 mL of Sterile Clarifier.

1. Fit a clean 25 mL graduated cylinder in the Blender’s container slot. Add the Moonmoss and run the Blender. Check the cylinder for **Moonmoss sap**.
2. Draw 1 mL from the red bottle with a 1 mL Dropper and add it to the cylinder. For a refined batch, add two full draws of Sterile Clarifier before heating.
3. Fit the cylinder on the Heating Plate and select **Low**. Once the actual temperature reaches **45 °C**, check for **Activated Moonmoss**. A refined batch also shows **Refined Moonmoss**.
4. Fit the cylinder in the [Chem Finisher](../chemistry-tools/), select **BroadGuard extract**, and output an extract. Each output uses 2 mL. Check its description for the sealing message and purity.
5. For bedside doses, seal one accepted extract into **2 BroadGuard packs** at the [sterile finishing bench](../sterile-bench/). For [Rhythm Buffer](../rhythm-buffer/), set the extract containing Refined Moonmoss aside for the compound recipe.
