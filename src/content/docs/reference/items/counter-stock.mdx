---
title: "Native stock, inspection, and procurement"
description: "Trade sealed stock, open a MED crate, or inspect the exact lot the clinic wants."
---

Keep sealed Medical Supplies for trade or sterile cartridges, or double-click and confirm when you want reagent bottles. Use the [base game's counter controls](../../systems/clinic-economy/#counter-trade) for retail, procurement, and barter. A spectrometer or native assessment checks your inventory lot. For a MED crate, bring the matching keycard and check the crate's actual contents after opening.

## Items and sources

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Water Testing Strip | An unused strip can document a relevant water concern on an eligible chart. |
| Medical Supplies (Common) and Medical Supplies (Restricted) | Open for the two clinic reagents, keep sealed for cartridges, or trade through native procurement. |
| Medical Bay Supply Crate and Disposable Keycard (Medical) | Use the matching card to open the crate. It may contain equipment and clinic supplies alongside ordinary loot. |
| Composition Spectrometer and Immunivax Assessment Guide | Inspect eligible stock using the original game appraisal tools. Use bedside tests to collect the patient's findings. |

| Item | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Birth Control Pill | Private native dispensing through the counter. |
