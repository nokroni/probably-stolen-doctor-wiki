---
title: "Portable ultrasound"
description: "Check a selected body region for an underlying finding."
---

Fit and power the ultrasound. In **Diagnostics**, select an eligible region and choose **Capture ultrasound**. Check the region named in the report. If the next action requires a scan of another site, select that site and capture it separately.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portable ultrasound | III | Scan the selected site for deeper findings. Needs its own power source or the medical power hub. |
