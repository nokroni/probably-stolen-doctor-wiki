---
title: "Microscope cradle"
description: "Prepare a powered surgery core and a sterile coupler for eligible tissue repair."
---

Fit the cradle alongside the powered surgery core and keep a Sterile finger coupler in stock. For an eligible hand injury, check the tissue box and obtain consent first. Follow the illustrated placement guide, then use **Check fingertip flow** on completion.
