---
title: "Clinic reagent bottles"
description: "Unpack Medical Supplies for the two clinic reagents, and keep full bottles for compounds."
---

Double-click one owned **Medical Supplies (Common)** or **Medical Supplies (Restricted)** and confirm. If you have room, you receive a full 10 mL bottle of each clinic reagent. Use the dropper for crop recipes. Check the item description before crafting a compound; it requires an entire, full bottle of the named reagent. Keep another sealed Medical Supplies pack if you also need cartridges.

## Items and sources

| Reagent bottle | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Bottle of Sterile Clarifier | Measure into eligible fictional crop mixtures. A full bottle is the carrier for Pressor Bridge and Epinephrine. |
| Bottle of Pulse Stabilizer | Measure into eligible fictional crop mixtures. A full bottle is the carrier for Rhythm Buffer. |
