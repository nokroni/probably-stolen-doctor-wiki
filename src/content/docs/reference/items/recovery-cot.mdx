---
title: "Recovery cot"
description: "Prepare a separate staffed space for monitored recovery."
---

Purchase and fit the cot from Grade III onward. When the chart offers a move to the recovery cot, finish its transfer checks. Once moved, verify that the sensors still read, check breathing and pulse, and take another cuff reading if shown. Complete the new observation window before signing.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recovery cot | III | Adds a staffed place for monitored recovery during eligible procedures. |
