---
title: "Bandages and dressings"
description: "Compare basic, hemostatic, and topical bandages for different wounds."
---

Select the examined body site and open the available dressing action. Choose the stocked bandage, hold the roll along the guide, and release once the progress bar is full. Check the site again afterward. For bleeding that needs temporary pressure first, complete that gesture before dressing. The bandage picker reflects the condition of the selected site.

## Items and sources

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Basic Bandage | Low-cost dressing for appropriate bleeding and burns. The starting clinic receives two. |
| Hemostatic Bandage | Faster wrapping for heavy bleeding. The starter clinic receives one. |
| Topical Medication Bandage | Dressing with an added shallow-wound benefit. The site and wound determine when it appears. |
