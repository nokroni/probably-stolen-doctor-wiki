---
title: "Station control module"
description: "Archive bedside observations and compare readings on the powered station."
---

Fit and power both the controller and the M4. When the station offers **Archive**, use it to save the current readings. Complete any checks listed next to a comparison or pressure flag before choosing it. The controller has its own compatible module bay.

## Items and sources

| Item | Grade | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Station control module | IV | Pairs with the M4 to record and compare observations at the procedure station. Accepts native modules. |

## Station controller modules

**Efficiency** adjusts the energy used by **Archive**. On a battery, +25% Efficiency reduces the cost from 1 unit to 0.75. The station also displays totals for **Performance** and **Quality**. Both are currently inactive on this device.
