- **When a critical patient needs fluids**, fit and power an infusion pump in the rack. Complete **Body exam**, then measure low blood pressure with the cuff or read it from a connected arterial line. If the chart also asks for electrolytes, collect a bedside chemistry sample. Select a usable forearm, open **Instruments → Treatment tray**, and select **ClearFlow infusion** and a lot. Recheck blood pressure after the infusion.
- **When the patient only needs electrolyte correction**, run bedside chemistry. If the result calls for correction, select a usable forearm and choose **Electrolyte support** in the treatment tray or the **ClearFlow · Electrolytes** bedside shortcut when shown. Follow the bedside prompts for reassessment.

For ordinary clinic visits, use the route offered at the bedside.

## Crafting recipe

**Ingredients and vessels for one portion**　1 Frostkelp strip, 1 Sugarpod kernel, 2 clean graduated cylinders, and a charged cell. For the stabilized route, measure **2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer for each pair of one strip and one kernel**.

1. Blend the strip and kernel together into the same clean 25 mL graduated cylinder. Check for both **Frostkelp sap** and **Sugarpod sap**.
2. A standard batch can go straight to the Evaporator. For the stabilized batch used in [Pressor Bridge](../pressor-bridge/), add the measured Pulse Stabilizer and heat on **Low**. The reaction starts at **40 °C** and requires an actual temperature **below 65 °C**. Take the cylinder off the plate when **Stabilized kelp salts** appear.
3. Fit the original cylinder in the Evaporator’s **Container** slot, a second empty cylinder in **Distillate**, and a charged cell in **Battery**. Leave it overnight. Check the item description on the original cylinder from **Container**. If it still lists Water, give the Evaporator more time. The distillate cylinder can stay empty when the machine removes water.
4. Fit the original cylinder with the dry residue in the [Chem Finisher](../chemistry-tools/) and select **ClearFlow extract**. Each output uses 2 mL.
5. One accepted extract makes **2 ClearFlow packs** at the [sterile finishing bench](../sterile-bench/). For Pressor Bridge, keep an extract containing Stabilized kelp salts. A sealed ClearFlow pack can go into the [Heparin](../heparin/) recipe.

Use one of each harvest item. An uneven ratio can drop the purity even when the extract still seals. [Crop ratio, purity, and test batches](../../systems/chemistry-quality/)

### One-pair test batch

In 0.17.109, with an unmodified Blender and Chem Finisher, one strip and one kernel yielded **2 accepted extracts at 88%** after drying, enough to seal **4 packs**. With 2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer added before heating, the matching pair yielded **3 extracts at 99%** after the salts formed and the mix dried, enough for **6 packs**. The distillate cylinder stayed empty while the water disappeared from the original cylinder. Check the original cylinder’s ingredient list to see when it is ready to finish.
