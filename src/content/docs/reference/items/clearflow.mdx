---
title: "ClearFlow"
description: "Use a ClearFlow lot through the eligible fluid or electrolyte action."
---

At a usable forearm, use the route offered by the chart. For an infusion, fit and power the pump and collect the requested pressure evidence first. Some charts use the bedside chemistry result to enable an electrolyte support action. After giving it, watch the pressure or follow the chart's requested repeat test.

## Items and sources

| Item | Bedside role | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ClearFlow | Sealed fluid pack for eligible infusion, circulation or electrolyte support. | Pharmacy or two packs from one ClearFlow extract. |

| Finished extract | In-game processing route |
| --- | --- |
| ClearFlow extract | Blend one Frostkelp strip and one Sugarpod kernel. Fit the vessel in the powered Evaporator with a separate empty distillate cylinder and leave it overnight. Finish the dry residue from the original vessel. For the stabilized fraction, add 2 mL of Pulse Stabilizer and warm on Low to 40 °C. Transfer to the Evaporator when stabilized kelp salts appear. |
