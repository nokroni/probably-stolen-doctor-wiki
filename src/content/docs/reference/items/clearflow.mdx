---
title: "ClearFlow"
description: "Use a ClearFlow lot through the eligible fluid or electrolyte action."
---

- **When a critical patient needs fluids**, fit and power an infusion pump in the rack. Complete **Body exam**, then measure low blood pressure with the cuff or read it from a connected arterial line. If the chart also asks for electrolytes, collect a bedside chemistry sample. Select a usable forearm, open **Instruments → Treatment tray**, and select **ClearFlow infusion** and a lot. Recheck blood pressure after the infusion.
- **When the patient only needs electrolyte correction**, run bedside chemistry. If the result calls for correction, select a usable forearm and choose **Electrolyte support** in the treatment tray or the **ClearFlow · Electrolytes** bedside shortcut when shown. Follow the bedside prompts for reassessment.

For ordinary clinic visits, use the route offered at the bedside.
