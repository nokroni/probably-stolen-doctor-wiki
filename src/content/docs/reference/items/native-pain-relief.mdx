title: "Native pain-relief injectors"
description: "Compare Oxymore and Nomore in monitored in-game pain relief."

Native purchases and other original acquisition routes supply these lots. From Grade II, assess the injury, attach ECG and finger probe, and select a usable forearm. Choose the indicated product in **Medicines**. The patient's reported reactions and current breathing can change the choice. Recheck comfort and respiration afterward.

## Items and sources

| Base-game item | Use in the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Oxymore Injector | Grade II supervised pain relief for assessed injuries. Attach ECG and finger probe and watch breathing after use. |
| Nomore Injector | Grade II alternative for monitored pain relief. Uses the native acquisition route. |
