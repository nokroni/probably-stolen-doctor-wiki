description: "Store medicines, sealed blood bags, and eligible treatment supplies."

Open the cabinet in the shop and transfer supplies into it. The bedside also checks unlocked owned stock in other eligible shop storage. If a confirmed lot is temporarily reserved for treatment, return to that visit to use or release it before moving the item.
